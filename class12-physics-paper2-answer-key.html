SECTION A — MCQ Answers 10 × 1 = 10
(i) Electric field between capacitor plates (E = σ/ε₀)
Answer: (A) σ/ε₀
For a parallel plate capacitor: E = σ/ε₀ where σ = surface charge density
(ii) Equivalent resistance of n resistors in parallel
Answer: (B) R/n
1/R_eq = n/R → R_eq = R/n (each has same resistance R)
(iii) Magnetic field at centre of circular loop
Answer: (B) μ₀I/2r
B = μ₀I/2r at centre of loop of radius r carrying current I
(iv) de Broglie wavelength λ = h/mv
Answer: (A) h/mv
Louis de Broglie: matter waves → λ = h/p = h/mv
(v) Transformer — output voltage doubled when turns doubled
Answer: (A) doubles
V_s/V_p = N_s/N_p → doubling N_s doubles V_s
(vi) Threshold frequency
Answer: (C) minimum frequency to cause photoelectric effect
Below threshold frequency ν₀, even high intensity light cannot eject electrons. φ = hν₀
(vii) p-type semiconductor — majority carriers
Answer: (B) holes
p-type formed by adding trivalent impurity (e.g. Boron) → creates holes → holes are majority carriers
(viii) Critical angle for glass-air (n = 1.5)
Answer: (C) 41.8°
sin C = 1/n = 1/1.5 = 0.667 → C = sin⁻¹(0.667) ≈ 41.8°
(ix) Nuclear radius R = R₀A^(1/3)
Answer: (A) R₀A^(1/3)
R = R₀ × A^(1/3) where R₀ = 1.2 fm, A = mass number
(x) NOT gate output for input 1
Answer: (B) 0
NOT gate: output = complement of input → input 1 → output 0
SECTION B — Very Short Answers 8 × 1 = 8
(i) State Biot-Savart law
The magnetic field dB at a point P due to a current element Idl is: dB = (μ₀/4π) × (Idl sinθ)/r²   Direction: right-hand rule
(ii) Define magnetic susceptibility
χ_m = M/H (ratio of magnetisation M to applied field H) Paramagnetic: χ > 0 | Diamagnetic: χ < 0 | Ferromagnetic: χ >> 1
(iii) State Faraday's first law of electromagnetic induction
Whenever the magnetic flux linked with a conductor changes, an EMF is induced in it. The induced EMF lasts as long as the flux is changing.
(iv) Define resonant frequency of LC circuit
f_r = 1/(2π√LC) At resonance: X_L = X_C → impedance is minimum = R (purely resistive)
(v) What are matter waves?
Matter waves (de Broglie waves) are waves associated with moving particles. λ = h/mv = h/p   Every moving particle has an associated wavelength.
(vi) What is rectification?
Rectification is the process of converting alternating current (AC) into direct current (DC) using a diode.
(vii) Define resolving power of a telescope
Resolving power is the ability of a telescope to distinguish between two closely spaced objects. RP = D/1.22λ (Rayleigh criterion) — higher aperture D gives better resolution
(viii) What is Heisenberg's uncertainty principle?
Δx · Δp ≥ h/4π It is impossible to simultaneously determine both position and momentum of a particle with perfect precision.
SECTION C — Short Answers (selected) 8 × 2 = 16
(i) Drift velocity of electrons
When electric field E is applied, electrons accelerate but collide frequently. Drift velocity v_d = eEτ/m where τ = relaxation time Current I = neAv_d where n = electron density, A = cross-sectional area
(iii) Displacement current
Maxwell introduced displacement current to complete Ampere's law: I_d = ε₀ dΦ_E/dt It exists in a capacitor gap when electric flux is changing. Produces magnetic field just like conduction current.
(v) Huygens' principle
1. Every point on a wavefront acts as a secondary source of new (spherical) wavelets. 2. The new wavefront at any later time is the surface tangent to all these secondary wavelets (envelope). Explains reflection, refraction and diffraction of light.
SECTION D — Medium Answers (selected) 6 × 3 = 18
(i) Gauss's law — field due to infinite line charge
By symmetry, E is radial and uniform around cylindrical surface Gaussian surface: cylinder of radius r, length l Gauss's law: ΦE = q_enc/ε₀ → E·2πrl = λl/ε₀ E = λ/(2πε₀r)   directed radially outward
(iii) Bohr's postulates
Postulate 1: Electrons orbit nucleus in fixed circular orbits without radiating energy. Postulate 2: Only orbits where angular momentum = nh/2π are allowed (n = 1, 2, 3...) Postulate 3: Energy is emitted/absorbed when electron jumps between orbits: hν = E₂ − E₁ Energy of nth orbit: E_n = −13.6/n² eV
SECTION E — Long Answers (selected) 4 × 4 = 16
(i) Gauss's law — proof and application
Statement: Total electric flux through any closed surface = q_enc/ε₀ Proof: For point charge q, E = kq/r² on sphere of radius r: Φ = ∮E·dA = E × 4πr² = (kq/r²) × 4πr² = q/ε₀ ✓ Infinite plane sheet: By symmetry, E is perpendicular to sheet on both sides Gaussian surface = pillbox (cylinder); only flat faces contribute: 2E·A = σA/ε₀ E = σ/2ε₀ (for infinite plane sheet)
(vi) Energy band diagrams
Conductor: Valence band and conduction band overlap → free electrons always available → conducts easily Semiconductor: Small band gap (~1 eV, e.g. Si = 1.1 eV) → at room temp, some electrons jump → partial conduction Insulator: Large band gap (>3 eV, e.g. diamond = 5.4 eV) → electrons cannot jump → no conduction Zener Diode Voltage Regulator: Connected in reverse bias across load. When input voltage rises, Zener conducts and clamps output voltage at V_Z = constant.
