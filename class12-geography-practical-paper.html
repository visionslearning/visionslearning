1. What is Mean? Write the structure of Mean. ▼
Mean: Mean is the arithmetic average of a set of data values. It is the most commonly used measure of central tendency.

Structure / Formula:
  • Mean (x̄) = Σx / n
  • Where Σx = sum of all values, n = total number of values
Steps to calculate Mean:
  • Step 1: Add all values to get Σx
  • Step 2: Count total values (n)
  • Step 3: Divide Σx by n to get Mean
Example: Data: 10, 20, 30 → Mean = (10+20+30)/3 = 60/3 = 20

3. Which are the Major Advantages by APP Survey? ▼
Major Advantages of App Survey:
  • Quick data collection — Large data can be collected in short time
  • Cost effective — No need for paper, printing or fieldwork expenses
  • Easy to reach — Can reach people across large areas through internet
  • Accurate data — Reduces human errors in data entry
  • Real-time results — Data is available immediately after submission
  • Eco-friendly — Paperless survey, reduces wastage
  • Easy analysis — Apps automatically create charts and graphs
4. Write the Structure of Divided Circle. ▼
Divided Circle (Pie Chart) — Structure:
  • Circle: A full circle representing the total (100% or 360°)
  • Sectors: Circle is divided into sectors/slices for each category
  • Angles: Each sector angle = (Value / Total) × 360°
  • Labels: Each sector is labelled with name, value and percentage
  • Legend: Different colours used for different sectors with a key
  • Title: Name of the chart is written at the top
Formula: Angle = (Component Value / Total Value) × 360°
5. Write the Benefit of Divided Circle. ▼
Benefits of Divided Circle (Pie Chart):
  • Easy to understand — visual representation is clear at a glance
  • Shows proportions — clearly shows how each part relates to whole
  • Attractive and visually appealing
  • Useful for comparing parts of a whole (like population by state, rainfall by month)
  • Simple to draw with compass and protractor
  • Widely used in geography for showing distribution data
6. What do you Mean by Survey? ▼
Survey: A survey is a method of collecting information or data from a group of people (sample) to understand characteristics, opinions, or facts about a larger population.

Types of Survey:
  • Field Survey — Collecting data by visiting places personally
  • Questionnaire Survey — Using printed or digital forms
  • Interview Survey — Face-to-face questions and answers
  • Online Survey — Using apps and websites
Purpose: To gather accurate, reliable data for research, planning, and decision-making.
7. Write the Uses of Population Pyramid. ▼
Uses of Population Pyramid:
  • Shows age and sex composition of a population at a glance
  • Helps in understanding demographic structure (young/old/working age)
  • Helps planners to plan schools, hospitals, jobs etc.
  • Shows whether population is growing, stable or declining
  • Useful for comparison between developed and developing countries
  • Helps predict future population trends
  • Shows effect of wars, famines, migration on population structure
Types: Expansive (developing), Constrictive (developed), Stationary (stable)
8. Which are the Various Ways to collect Statistical Information? ▼
Various Ways to Collect Statistical Information:
  • Direct Personal Interview — Interviewer collects data face-to-face
  • Indirect Oral Interview — Data collected from persons who know about the subject
  • Questionnaire Method — Printed forms sent by post or given directly
  • Online Survey / App Survey — Digital forms via Google Forms, apps
  • Field Observation — Direct observation and recording in the field
  • Secondary Sources — Government reports, census data, newspapers, internet
  • Telephone/SMS Survey — Collecting data via phone calls or messages
  • Focus Group Discussion — Group interview for qualitative data
