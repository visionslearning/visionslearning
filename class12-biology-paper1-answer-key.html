Q.2 (ii) [1 Mark]
Define test cross. Why is it performed?
DefinitionCrossing a phenotypically dominant organism with homozygous recessive (aa) to determine if it is AA or Aa.
PurposeIf any offspring show recessive phenotype → parent is Aa (heterozygous). If all dominant → parent is AA.
Q.2 (iii) [1 Mark]
What is a codon? Give one example.
DefinitionA codon is a sequence of 3 consecutive nucleotides on mRNA that codes for a specific amino acid.
ExampleAUG = start codon (methionine). UAA, UAG, UGA = stop codons. Total 64 codons for 20 amino acids.
Q.2 (v) [1 Mark]
What is photophosphorylation? State its type.
DefinitionSynthesis of ATP using light energy in the thylakoid membranes during photosynthesis.
TypesCyclic: Only PS I involved, only ATP produced (no NADPH, no O₂). Non-cyclic: Both PS I and PS II, produces ATP + NADPH + O₂.

What is lac operon? Name the structural genes.
Lac operon: I-P-O-lacZ-lacY-lacA. Lactose inactivates repressor → all 3 genes expressed

Q.3 (ii) [2 Marks]
Explain Mendel's law of dominance with a suitable example.
Monohybrid cross showing dominance of Tall over Dwarf
StatementWhen two contrasting forms are crossed, only the dominant character appears in F₁. The other (recessive) is hidden but reappears in F₂ in 3:1 ratio.
ExampleTall (TT) × Dwarf (tt) → F₁: All Tall (Tt). F₂: 3 Tall : 1 Dwarf. Tallness (T) is dominant over dwarfness (t).
Q.3 (vii) [2 Marks]
Explain Bt toxin and its mode of action in pest control.
Bt toxin mode of action: inactive protoxin → activated by alkaline insect midgut → cell lysis → insect death
Bt ToxinCry protein produced by Bacillus thuringiensis (soil bacterium), encoded by cry genes.
Mode of Action 1. Insect ingests Bt plant → protoxin activated by alkaline pH of midgut 2. Active Cry protein binds to specific receptors on midgut epithelial cells 3. Creates pores in cell membrane → cells swell and lyse → insect dies 4. Safe for humans (we lack the specific midgut receptor)
Q.3 (xi) [2 Marks]
Describe the Meselson-Stahl experiment. What did it prove?
Meselson-Stahl: ¹⁵N→¹⁴N medium: Gen1=hybrid, Gen2=hybrid+light (proves semi-conservative)
Experiment E. coli grown in ¹⁵N (heavy) → transferred to ¹⁴N (light) medium. After 1 generation: CsCl gradient → ONE band at intermediate density (¹⁵N/¹⁴N hybrid) After 2 generations: TWO bands — one hybrid, one light (¹⁴N/¹⁴N)
Conclusion
DNA replication is SEMI-CONSERVATIVE — each new molecule has one original + one new strand

Q.4 (iv) [3 Marks]
Explain the light reactions of photosynthesis — photosystems, ETC and ATP synthesis.
Z-scheme: electrons flow from H₂O through PS II → ETC → PS I → NADP⁺
PS II (P680)Absorbs 680 nm. Water photolysis: 2H₂O → 4H⁺ + 4e⁻ + O₂. Electrons → ETC (PQ → Cyt b6f → PC). Proton gradient → ATP.
PS I (P700)Absorbs 700 nm. Electrons → ferredoxin → NADP⁺ reductase → NADPH.
Products
ATP + NADPH + O₂ → fed into Calvin cycle (dark reactions)
Q.4 (vii) [3 Marks]
Explain biomagnification with DDT as an example.
Biomagnification: DDT increases from 0.003 to 25 ppm up the food chain
DefinitionProgressive increase in concentration of a non-biodegradable substance at successive trophic levels in a food chain.
DDT Example DDT sprayed on water → phytoplankton (0.003 ppm) → zooplankton (0.04) → small fish (0.5) → large fish (2) → fish-eating birds (25 ppm) Effect: DDT causes eggshell thinning in predatory birds (osprey, pelicans, bald eagle) → egg breakage → population crashes.
