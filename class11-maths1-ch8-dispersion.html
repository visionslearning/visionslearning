Theory — Measures of Dispersion

What is Dispersion?

"The degree to which numerical data tend to spread about an average value is called the variation or dispersion of the data." — Spiegel

Mean, Median, Mode tell us the central value but not the spread. Dispersion fills that gap.

Measures of Dispersion

  • (i) Range
  • (ii) Variance
  • (iii) Standard Deviation (S.D.)
  • (iv) Coefficient of Variation (C.V.)
📏
8.1 Range

Definition

Range = Largest Value − Smallest Value

Range = L − S

For grouped data: L = Upper limit of highest class, S = Lower limit of lowest class.

Uses: Stock market, Mean temperature calculations.

σ²
8.2 Variance and Standard Deviation

Variance — Definition

Variance = Arithmetic mean of squares of all deviations from mean. Denoted by Var(X) or σ².

Var(X) = σ² = (1/n)·Σ(xᵢ − x̄)² = Σxᵢ²/n − x̄²

Standard Deviation (S.D.)

S.D. = Positive square root of Variance. Denoted by σ.

σ = √Var(X)

Formulas — All Cases

CaseVariance Formula
Raw dataσ² = Σxᵢ²/n − x̄²
Ungrouped freq.σ² = Σfᵢxᵢ²/N − x̄²
Grouped freq. (step deviation)σ² = h²·(Σfᵢuᵢ²/N − ū²) where u = (x−A)/h
🔗
8.3 Standard Deviation for Combined Data

Combined Mean and S.D.

x̄c = (n₁x̄₁ + n₂x̄₂)/(n₁+n₂)

σc = √[ n₁(σ₁²+d₁²) + n₂(σ₂²+d₂²) ] / (n₁+n₂)

where d₁ = x̄₁ − x̄c   and   d₂ = x̄₂ − x̄c

%
8.3.1 Coefficient of Variation (C.V.)

Definition and Formula

C.V. = (σ/x̄) × 100
  • Smaller C.V. → More consistent / homogeneous / compact
  • Larger C.V. → More heterogeneous / variable

Exercise 8.2 — Variance and S.D.
Q.1
Find variance and S.D. for: 7, 11, 2, 4, 9, 6, 3, 7, 11, 2, 5, 8, 3, 6, 8, 8, 2, 6
Given
Data: 7,11,2,4,9,6,3,7,11,2,5,8,3,6,8,8,2,6   n = 18
Step 1: Σxᵢ
Σxᵢ = 7+11+2+4+9+6+3+7+11+2+5+8+3+6+8+8+2+6 = 108
Step 2: x̄
x̄ = 108/18 = 6
Step 3: Σxᵢ²
Σxᵢ² = 49+121+4+16+81+36+9+49+121+4+25+64+9+36+64+64+4+36 = 792
Formula
Var(X) = Σxᵢ²/n − x̄²
Step 4
Var(X) = 792/18 − 6² = 44 − 36 = 8
Step 5
S.D. = √8 = 2√2 ≈ 2.83
∴ Var = 8,
S.D. = 2√2 ≈ 2.83

Find mean, variance and S.D.: Classes 10-20, 20-30, ..., 90-100. Freq: 7,14,6,13,9,15,11,10,15

Grouped data. Use step deviation: A = 55 (middle class), h = 10. N = 100
Step 1: Mid pts
15, 25, 35, 45, 55, 65, 75, 85, 95

−4, −3, −2, −1, 0, 1, 2, 3, 4

Q.8
Mean of 5 observations is 4.8, variance is 6.56. Three observations are 1, 3, 8. Find the other two.
Given
n=5, x̄=4.8, σ²=6.56, three values: 1, 3, 8. Let other two be a and b.
Step 1: Sum
Σx = n·x̄ = 5 × 4.8 = 24
Step 2
1+3+8+a+b = 24 → a+b = 12 …(I)
Step 3: Σx²
σ² = Σx²/n − x̄² → 6.56 = Σx²/5 − (4.8)² → Σx² = 5(6.56+23.04) = 5×29.6 = 148
Step 4
1+9+64+a²+b² = 148 → a²+b² = 74 …(II)
Step 5
(a+b)² = a²+2ab+b² → 144 = 74+2ab → ab = 35 …(III)
Step 6
a and b are roots of: t² − 12t + 35 = 0 → (t−7)(t−5) = 0
∴ Other two
= 5 and 7

Exercise 8.3 — Combined S.D. and C.V.
Q.1
Two samples: n₁=60, n₂=120, x̄₁=35.4, x̄₂=30.9, σ₁=4, σ₂=5. Find combined S.D.
Given
n₁=60, n₂=120, x̄₁=35.4, x̄₂=30.9, σ₁=4, σ₂=5
Step 1: x̄c
x̄c = (60×35.4 + 120×30.9)/(60+120) = (2124+3708)/180 = 5832/180 = 32.4
Step 2: d₁, d₂
d₁ = 35.4−32.4 = 3    d₂ = 30.9−32.4 = −1.5
Formula
σc = √[ n₁(σ₁²+d₁²) + n₂(σ₂²+d₂²) ] / (n₁+n₂)
Step 3
= √[ 60(16+9) + 120(25+2.25) ] / 180 = √[ 60×25 + 120×27.25 ] / 180
Step 4
= √[ 1500 + 3270 ] / 180 = √(4770/180) = √26.5 ≈ 5.15
∴ Combined S.D.
≈ 5.15

d₁ = 13−15.4 = −2.4    d₂ = 17−15.4 = 1.6

∴ Combined S.D.

Let's Remember — Quick Revision

All Key Formulas at a Glance

FormulaExpression
RangeR = L − S
Variance (raw)σ² = Σxᵢ²/n − x̄²
Variance (freq)σ² = Σfᵢxᵢ²/N − x̄²
Step deviationu=(x−A)/h; σx² = h²·σu²
S.D.σ = √Var(X)
Combined Meanx̄c = (n₁x̄₁+n₂x̄₂)/(n₁+n₂)
Combined S.D.σc = √[n₁(σ₁²+d₁²)+n₂(σ₂²+d₂²)]/(n₁+n₂)
C.V.C.V. = (σ/x̄) × 100

💡 Key Points to Remember

  • Smaller C.V. → More consistent / homogeneous
  • Larger C.V. → More variable / heterogeneous
  • Variance is independent of change of origin (adding/subtracting constant)
  • Variance is NOT independent of change of scale — multiplying by h: σx = h·σu
  • S.D. is always non-negative
  • For any two numbers, S.D. = half the range
