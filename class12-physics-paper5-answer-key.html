SECTION A — MCQ Answers 10 × 1 = 10
(i) Principle of superposition of electric fields
Answer: (A) Net field = vector sum of individual fields
E⃗_net = E⃗₁ + E⃗₂ + E⃗₃ + ... (vector addition)
(ii) Resistance of a wire — when length doubled and area halved
Answer: (D) 4R
R = ρL/A → R' = ρ(2L)/(A/2) = 4ρL/A = 4R
(iii) Lenz's law statement
Answer: (B) Induced current opposes the change in magnetic flux
Lenz's law: direction of induced current is such that it opposes the cause producing it (conservation of energy)
(iv) Visible light wavelength range
Answer: (B) 400 nm to 700 nm
Violet: ~400 nm → Red: ~700 nm  |  VIBGYOR order
(v) For a proton: charge-to-mass ratio (q/m)
Answer: (A) 9.58×10⁷ C/kg
q/m = 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ / 1.67×10⁻²⁷ ≈ 9.58×10⁷ C/kg (specific charge of proton)
(vi) γ-rays are
Answer: (C) high energy electromagnetic radiation
γ-rays: λ < 0.01 nm, no charge, no mass, highest penetrating power in nuclear radiation
(vii) Frequency in common emitter amplifier — output vs input
Answer: (A) same
Amplifier amplifies amplitude, NOT frequency. Output frequency = input frequency.
(viii) In YDSE, dark fringe position x_n
Answer: (B) x_n = (2n−1)λD/2d
Dark fringe at: x = (2n−1)λD/2d   (n = 1, 2, 3...)
(ix) Speed of EM waves in vacuum
Answer: (A) 3×10⁸ m/s
c = 1/√(μ₀ε₀) = 3×10⁸ m/s (derived by Maxwell)
(x) Intrinsic semiconductor at room temperature
Answer: (B) number of holes = number of free electrons
Thermal energy breaks bonds → each broken bond creates one electron AND one hole → n_e = n_h in intrinsic semiconductor
SECTION B — Very Short Answers 8 × 1 = 8
(i) What is electric dipole moment?
Electric dipole moment (p) = product of either charge and separation between charges. p⃗ = q × 2a⃗   Unit: C·m   Direction: negative to positive charge
(ii) Define temperature coefficient of resistance
α = increase in resistance per unit original resistance per degree rise in temperature. R_T = R₀(1 + αT)   Unit: per °C or per K
(iii) State Fleming's left-hand rule
Stretch thumb, index finger and middle finger of left hand mutually perpendicular: Forefinger → B (magnetic field) | Middle finger → I (current) | Thumb → F (force on conductor)
(iv) What is electromagnetic induction?
The phenomenon of generation of EMF (and hence current) in a conductor due to a change in magnetic flux linked with it is called electromagnetic induction (Faraday, 1831).
(v) Define magnifying power of a simple microscope
m = 1 + D/f   (when image at near point D = 25 cm) m = D/f   (when image at infinity)   where f = focal length of lens
(vi) What is pair production?
When a high-energy gamma photon (E > 1.02 MeV) passes near a nucleus, it converts into an electron-positron pair. γ → e⁻ + e⁺ (pair production)
(vii) What is the use of a Zener diode?
Zener diode is used as a voltage regulator — it maintains a constant output voltage (V_Z) despite variations in input voltage or load current.
(viii) State the laws of refraction (Snell's law)
1. Incident ray, refracted ray and normal at point of incidence are coplanar. 2. n₁ sinθ₁ = n₂ sinθ₂   (Snell's law)
SECTION C — Short Answers (selected) 8 × 2 = 16
(i) Van de Graaff generator
Principle: Charge given to hollow conductor resides on outer surface (Gauss's law). Charge is continuously sprayed onto inner surface of large dome via a belt → accumulates on outer surface → high potential (millions of volts). Use: Nuclear physics research (particle accelerator)
(iii) Cyclotron working
Positive ion placed between two D-shaped chambers (dees) in magnetic field B. Electric field E accelerates ion across gap; B makes it move in semicircle. Ion gains energy each time it crosses the gap → spirals outward. Max velocity: v_max = qBR/m  |  KE = q²B²R²/2m
(vii) Laser — properties
LASER = Light Amplification by Stimulated Emission of Radiation Properties: (1) Coherent (2) Monochromatic (3) Directional (4) Very high intensity Uses: Surgery, CD/DVD, fibre optics, range-finding, cutting/welding
SECTION D — Medium Answers (selected) 6 × 3 = 18
(ii) Ohm's law on microscopic level
I = neAv_d   where n = free electron density, e = charge, A = area, v_d = drift velocity v_d = eEτ/m   (E = electric field, τ = relaxation time) J = I/A = nev_d = (ne²τ/m)E = σE σ = ne²τ/m   (conductivity) → confirms Ohm's law J = σE
(v) Nuclear fusion
Fusion = combining two light nuclei to form a heavier nucleus with release of energy. ₁H² + ₁H² → ₂He⁴ + energy (Q ≈ 24 MeV) Requires temperature ~10⁷ K (thermonuclear reaction) → occurs in stars. Energy per nucleon > fission. Basis of H-bomb and proposed fusion reactors (ITER).
