Q.1 Read the following passage and do all the activities. [12 Marks]
A₁. Read and answer: (Do before reading the passage)
Name any two things that you would do to protect the environment.

Passage:
Trees are the lungs of the Earth. They absorb carbon dioxide and release oxygen, making the air fresh and breathable. A single tree can absorb up to 48 pounds of carbon dioxide per year. In addition to cleaning the air, trees also help in reducing soil erosion. Their roots hold the soil firmly together, preventing it from being washed away by rain. Trees provide shade and reduce the temperature around them, making cities cooler. Forests also serve as the habitat of millions of animals and birds.

However, deforestation is a major problem today. Large areas of forests are being cleared for agriculture, construction and mining. When trees are cut, wildlife loses its home and carbon dioxide increases in the atmosphere, contributing to global warming. Planting trees and protecting existing forests is the need of the hour. Every citizen must take responsibility for planting at least one tree in their lifetime.

Many countries have started campaigns for tree plantation. India's 'Van Mahotsav' is celebrated every year in the first week of July. Children, students and government officials participate in this festival by planting saplings. Schools have adopted the concept of 'Green School' where students take care of the school garden and learn to love nature.

And sorry I could not travel both

To where it bent in the undergrowth;

And having perhaps the better claim,

The scientist sat alone in his laboratory, surrounded by books and instruments. He had been working for years on a formula that could change the world. His colleagues had given up hope, but he remained persistent. "One day," he would say, "this discovery will help millions of people." That day finally came.
