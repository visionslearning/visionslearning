Q.1 — Select and write the correct answer   [4 Marks — 1M each]
(i) Which mirror is used as a rear-view mirror in vehicles? [1]
(A) Concave mirror (B) Convex mirror (C) Plane mirror (D) Parabolic mirror
(ii) The critical angle for a glass-air interface is 42°. The refractive index of glass is ______. [1]
(A) 1.33 (B) 1.49 (C) 1.60 (D) 2.00
(iii) A convex lens has power +2 D and a concave lens has power −1 D. The power of their combination is ______. [1]
(A) +1 D (B) −1 D (C) +3 D (D) +2 D
(iv) Total internal reflection occurs when light travels from ______. [1]
(A) rarer to denser at any angle (B) denser to rarer at any angle (C) rarer to denser at i > θ_c (D) denser to rarer at i > θ_c
Q.2 — Answer the following   [4 Marks — 1M each]
(i) Write the mirror formula. [1]
(ii) State any two applications of total internal reflection. [1]
(iii) Define power of a lens. Write its SI unit. [1]
(iv) What is the angle of deviation in a prism? Write its formula. [1]
Q.3 — Short Answer I (2M each)   [4 Marks — Attempt any TWO]
Attempt any TWO of the following (A), (B), (C).

— OR —
(B) Distinguish between a concave mirror and a convex mirror. (Any two points each) [2]
— OR —
(C) What is total internal reflection? State the two conditions for TIR to occur. Give one application. [2]
Q.4 — Short Answer II (3M each)   [6 Marks — Attempt any TWO]
Attempt any TWO of the following (A), (B), (C).
(A) State the Lens Maker's formula. A convex lens (n = 1.5) has radii of curvature R₁ = 20 cm and R₂ = −20 cm. Find its focal length and power. [3]
— OR —
(B) Derive the formula for the refractive index of a prism in terms of angle of prism A and angle of minimum deviation Dm. [3]
— OR —
(C) Derive the mirror formula: 1/f = 1/v + 1/u for a concave mirror. Draw a neat labelled diagram. [3]
