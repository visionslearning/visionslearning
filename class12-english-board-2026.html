Q.1B — Non-Textual Grammar
B1 — Do as Directed (MCQ — choose correct option)3 Marks
❓ Questions

(1) Rewrite in Positive degree: "Gitesh is the best football player in the college."

(2) Use 'as soon as': "Rohit Sharma entered the ground. He got a warm welcome."

(3) Indirect speech: Swarali said to her mother, "Do you like my sketch?"

✅ Answers
(1) (D) Very few football players in the college are as good as Gitesh.
(2) (C) As soon as Rohit Sharma enters the ground, he gets a warm welcome.
(3) (D) Swarali asked her mother if she liked her sketch.
1 mark each = 3 marks
B2 — Spot the Error1 Mark
❓ Question

"We did not attended the party last night."

✅ Answer
Error: attended → attend
Correct: "We did not attend the party last night."
💡 Rule: After "did not", always use the base form of the verb (V1), not past tense (V2).
1 mark

Exam Strategy & Tips
How to score 70+ in English Board Paper
SectionStrategy
Section I — ProseRead extract carefully before answering. For grammar MCQs — eliminate wrong options first. Vocabulary: answers are always from the extract itself.
Section II — PoetryMemorise 2–3 figures of speech from each poem with examples. Write appreciation in a fixed template — don't skip any component.
Section III — WritingWrite in the correct FORMAT first — even if content is average, format marks are guaranteed. Practise one of each type before the exam.
Section IV — NovelRevise character sketches, plot summary, and 5–6 important quotes. Write subjective answers in 50–80 words — not more.
Grammar (B1/B2)B2 Spot the Error: check subject-verb agreement, tense, preposition. After "did/does/do" → always V1 (base form).
⭐ B2 Most Common Error Types in Board Papers:
1. "did not attended" → should be "did not attend" (base form after did not)
2. "has climb" → should be "has climbed" (present perfect = has/have + V3)
3. "peoples" → should be "people" (no plural with 's' for people)
4. "they was" → should be "they were" (plural subject → were)
