⚡ 3 Fundamental Identities — Use in Every Problem
1. sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 → sin²θ = 1 − cos²θ | cos²θ = 1 − sin²θ
2. 1 + tan²θ = sec²θ → tan²θ = sec²θ − 1 | sec²θ − tan²θ = 1
3. 1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ → cot²θ = cosec²θ − 1 | cosec²θ − cot²θ = 1
★ Reciprocal Relations
sin θ = 1/cosec θ  |  cos θ = 1/sec θ  |  tan θ = 1/cot θ
tan θ = sin θ / cos θ  |  sin θ = tan θ × cos θ  |  cot θ = cos θ / sin θ
🗺️ Polar Co-ordinates
Cartesian (x,y) → Polar (r,θ): r = √(x²+y²)  ,  tanθ = y/x
Polar (r,θ) → Cartesian (x,y): x = r cosθ  ,  y = r sinθ
