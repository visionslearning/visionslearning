⚡ Fundamental Identities
1. sin²θ + cos²θ = 1  →  sin²θ = 1 − cos²θ  |  cos²θ = 1 − sin²θ
2. 1 + tan²θ = sec²θ  →  tan²θ = sec²θ − 1  |  sec²θ − tan²θ = 1

Q 5If 2 sin²θ + 3 sin θ = 0, find the permissible values of cos θ.
Solution 2 sin²θ + 3 sin θ = 0 Taking sin θ common: sin θ (2 sin θ + 3) = 0 Either sin θ = 0   or   2 sin θ + 3 = 0 Either sin θ = 0   or   sin θ = −3/2 But   −1 ≤ sin θ ≤ 1,   so sin θ = −3/2 is not valid. ∴ sin θ = 0 cos²θ = 1 − sin²θ = 1 − 0 = 1 cos θ = +1   or   cos θ = −1
Q 6If 2 cos²θ − 11 cos θ + 5 = 0, find possible values of cos θ.
Solution 2 cos²θ − 11 cos θ + 5 = 0 Factorising: 2 cos²θ − 10 cos θ − cos θ + 5 = 0 2 cos θ (cos θ − 5) − 1(cos θ − 5) = 0 (2 cos θ − 1)(cos θ − 5) = 0 cos θ = 1/2   or   cos θ = 5 But cos θ = 5 is not valid since   −1 ≤ cos θ ≤ 1 cos θ = 1/2
Q 7Find the acute angle θ such that 2 cos²θ = 3 sin θ.
Solution 2 cos²θ = 3 sin θ Replacing cos²θ = 1 − sin²θ: 2 (1 − sin²θ) = 3 sin θ 2 − 2 sin²θ = 3 sin θ 2 sin²θ + 3 sin θ − 2 = 0 Factorising: (2 sin θ − 1)(sin θ + 2) = 0 sin θ = 1/2   or   sin θ = −2 (rejected, not in range) sin θ = 1/2 θ = 30°
Q 8Find the acute angle θ such that 5 tan²θ + 3 = 9 sec θ.
Solution 5 tan²θ + 3 = 9 sec θ Replacing tan²θ = sec²θ − 1: 5 (sec²θ − 1) + 3 = 9 sec θ 5 sec²θ − 5 + 3 = 9 sec θ 5 sec²θ − 9 sec θ − 2 = 0 Factorising: (5 sec θ + 1)(sec θ − 2) = 0 sec θ = −1/5 (rejected, |sec θ| ≥ 1)   or   sec θ = 2 sec θ = 2   →   cos θ = 1/2 θ = 60°
Q 9Find sin θ such that 3 cos θ + 4 sin θ = 4.
Solution 3 cos θ + 4 sin θ = 4 3 cos θ = 4 − 4 sin θ Squaring both sides: 9 cos²θ = (4 − 4 sin θ)² 9 (1 − sin²θ) = 16 − 32 sin θ + 16 sin²θ 9 − 9 sin²θ = 16 − 32 sin θ + 16 sin²θ 25 sin²θ − 32 sin θ + 7 = 0 Factorising: (25 sin θ − 7)(sin θ − 1) = 0 sin θ = 7/25   or   sin θ = 1 sin θ = 7/25   or   sin θ = 1
Q 10If cosec θ + cot θ = 5, evaluate sec θ.
Solution Given:   cosec θ + cot θ = 5   ...(1) We know:   cosec²θ − cot²θ = 1 (cosec θ + cot θ)(cosec θ − cot θ) = 1 5 × (cosec θ − cot θ) = 1 cosec θ − cot θ = 1/5   ...(2) Adding (1) and (2): 2 cosec θ = 5 + 1/5 = 26/5 cosec θ = 13/5 sin θ = 5/13 cos²θ = 1 − sin²θ = 1 − 25/169 = 144/169 cos θ = 12/13 sec θ = 1/cos θ = 13/12

Q15 (i)Prove: (1 + tan²A)(1 + 1/tan²A) = 1/(sin²A − sin⁴A)
Solution LHS = (1 + tan²A)(1 + cot²A) sec²A × cosec²A     [∵ 1+tan²A=sec²A, 1+cot²A=cosec²A] 1/cos²A × 1/sin²A 1/(sin²A cos²A) RHS = 1/(sin²A − sin⁴A) 1/[sin²A(1 − sin²A)] 1/(sin²A cos²A) LHS = RHS ✓
Q15 (ii)Prove: (cos²A − 1)(cot²A + 1) = −1
Solution LHS = (cos²A − 1)(cot²A + 1) Now, cos²A − 1 = −sin²A    and    cot²A + 1 = cosec²A (−sin²A) × cosec²A −sin²A × (1/sin²A) −1 LHS = −1 = RHS ✓
Q15 (iii)Prove: (sin θ + sec θ)² + (cos θ + cosec θ)² = (1 + sec θ cosec θ)²
Solution LHS = sin²θ + 2 sin θ sec θ + sec²θ + cos²θ + 2 cos θ cosec θ + cosec²θ (sin²θ + cos²θ) + (sec²θ + cosec²θ) + 2 sin θ sec θ + 2 cos θ cosec θ 1 + sec²θ cosec²θ + 2(sin θ/cos θ + cos θ/sin θ) Now, sin θ/cos θ + cos θ/sin θ = (sin²θ+cos²θ)/(sin θ cos θ) = 1/(sin θ cos θ) = sec θ cosec θ 1 + sec²θ cosec²θ + 2 sec θ cosec θ (1 + sec θ cosec θ)² LHS = RHS ✓
Q15 (iv)Prove: (1 + cot θ − cosec θ)(1 + tan θ + sec θ) = 2
Solution LHS = [(sin θ + cos θ − 1)/sin θ] × [(cos θ + sin θ + 1)/cos θ] [(sin θ + cos θ)² − 1²] / (sin θ cos θ) [sin²θ + 2 sin θ cos θ + cos²θ − 1] / (sin θ cos θ) [1 + 2 sin θ cos θ − 1] / (sin θ cos θ) 2 sin θ cos θ / (sin θ cos θ) 2 LHS = 2 = RHS ✓
Q15 (v)Prove: tan³θ/(1+tan²θ) + cot³θ/(1+cot²θ) = sec θ cosec θ − 2 sin θ cos θ
Solution 1 + tan²θ = sec²θ   →   tan³θ/sec²θ = sin³θ/cos θ 1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ   →   cot³θ/cosec²θ = cos³θ/sin θ LHS = sin³θ/cos θ + cos³θ/sin θ (sin⁴θ + cos⁴θ) / (sin θ cos θ) sin⁴θ + cos⁴θ = (sin²θ + cos²θ)² − 2 sin²θ cos²θ = 1 − 2 sin²θ cos²θ (1 − 2 sin²θ cos²θ) / (sin θ cos θ) 1/(sin θ cos θ) − 2 sin θ cos θ sec θ cosec θ − 2 sin θ cos θ LHS = RHS ✓
Q15 (vi)Prove: 1/(sec θ+tan θ) − 1/cos θ = 1/cos θ − 1/(sec θ−tan θ)
Solution Multiply 1/(sec θ+tan θ) by (sec θ−tan θ)/(sec θ−tan θ): 1/(sec θ+tan θ) = (sec θ−tan θ)/(sec²θ − tan²θ) = sec θ − tan θ   [∵ sec²θ−tan²θ=1] LHS = (sec θ − tan θ) − sec θ = −tan θ Similarly, 1/(sec θ−tan θ) = sec θ + tan θ RHS = sec θ − (sec θ + tan θ) = −tan θ LHS = −tan θ = RHS ✓
Q15 (vii)Prove: sin θ/(1+cos θ) + (1+cos θ)/sin θ = 2 cosec θ
Solution LHS = [sin²θ + (1+cos θ)²] / [sin θ(1+cos θ)] Numerator = sin²θ + 1 + 2 cos θ + cos²θ (sin²θ + cos²θ) + 1 + 2 cos θ 1 + 1 + 2 cos θ 2 + 2 cos θ = 2(1 + cos θ) LHS = 2(1+cos θ) / [sin θ(1+cos θ)] 2/sin θ 2 cosec θ LHS = 2 cosec θ = RHS ✓
Q15 (viii)Prove: tan θ/(sec θ−1) = (sec θ+1)/tan θ
Solution LHS = tan θ/(sec θ−1) Multiply numerator and denominator by (sec θ + 1): tan θ(sec θ+1) / [(sec θ−1)(sec θ+1)] tan θ(sec θ+1) / (sec²θ − 1) ∵ sec²θ − 1 = tan²θ tan θ(sec θ+1) / tan²θ (sec θ+1) / tan θ LHS = (sec θ+1)/tan θ = RHS ✓
Q15 (ix)Prove: cot θ/(cosec θ−1) = (cosec θ+1)/cot θ
Solution LHS = cot θ/(cosec θ−1) Multiply numerator and denominator by (cosec θ + 1): cot θ(cosec θ+1) / [(cosec θ−1)(cosec θ+1)] cot θ(cosec θ+1) / (cosec²θ − 1) ∵ cosec²θ − 1 = cot²θ cot θ(cosec θ+1) / cot²θ (cosec θ+1) / cot θ LHS = (cosec θ+1)/cot θ = RHS ✓
Q15 (x)Prove: (sec A + cos A)(sec A − cos A) = tan²A + sin²A
Solution LHS = (sec A + cos A)(sec A − cos A) sec²A − cos²A     [(a+b)(a−b) = a² − b²] Now, sec²A = 1 + tan²A   and   cos²A = 1 − sin²A (1 + tan²A) − (1 − sin²A) 1 + tan²A − 1 + sin²A tan²A + sin²A LHS = tan²A + sin²A = RHS ✓
Q15 (xi)Prove: 1 + 3 cosec²θ · cot²θ + cot⁶θ = cosec⁶θ
Solution Start with RHS: cosec⁶θ = (cosec²θ)³ ∵ cosec²θ = 1 + cot²θ (1 + cot²θ)³ Using (a + b)³ = a³ + 3a²b + 3ab² + b³: 1 + 3 cot²θ + 3 cot⁴θ + cot⁶θ 1 + 3 cot²θ(1 + cot²θ) + cot⁶θ 1 + 3 cot²θ · cosec²θ + cot⁶θ = LHS ✓
Q15 (xii)Prove: (1−sec θ+tan θ)/(1+sec θ−tan θ) = (sec θ+tan θ−1)/(sec θ+tan θ+1)
Solution Numerator of LHS × (sec θ+tan θ+1): (1+tan θ−sec θ)(1+tan θ+sec θ) = (1+tan θ)² − sec²θ 1 + 2 tan θ + tan²θ − sec²θ ∵ tan²θ − sec²θ = −1 1 + 2 tan θ − 1 = 2 tan θ Denominator of LHS × (sec θ+tan θ+1): (1+sec θ−tan θ)(1+sec θ+tan θ) = (1+sec θ)² − tan²θ 1 + 2 sec θ + sec²θ − tan²θ ∵ sec²θ − tan²θ = 1 1 + 2 sec θ + 1 = 2(1 + sec θ) LHS = 2 tan θ / 2(1+sec θ) = tan θ/(1+sec θ) Similarly RHS simplifies to tan θ/(1+sec θ) LHS = RHS ✓
