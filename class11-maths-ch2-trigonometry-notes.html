🔗 Reciprocal & Quotient Relations
sin θ × cosec θ = 1  |  cos θ × sec θ = 1  |  tan θ × cot θ = 1
tan θ = sin θ / cos θ  |  cot θ = cos θ / sin θ
sin θ = tan θ × cos θ
⚡ Fundamental Identities (Most Important)
Identity 1 : sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
→ sin²θ = 1 − cos²θ | cos²θ = 1 − sin²θ

Identity 2 : 1 + tan²θ = sec²θ (θ ≠ π/2)
→ sec²θ − tan²θ = 1 | tan²θ = sec²θ − 1

Identity 3 : 1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ (θ ≠ 0)
→ cosec²θ − cot²θ = 1 | cot²θ = cosec²θ − 1
★ Important Derived Relations
(sec θ + tan θ)(sec θ − tan θ) = 1
(cosec θ + cot θ)(cosec θ − cot θ) = 1
cos⁶θ + sin⁶θ = 1 − 3sin²θ cos²θ

Given
