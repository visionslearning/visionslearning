State Ohm's Law. Write its formula and state the conditions for it to hold.

What is a covalent bond? Why does carbon form covalent bonds?
Covalent bond: A bond formed by sharing of electron pairs between two atoms.

Why carbon: Carbon has 4 valence electrons. To achieve stable configuration it needs to gain or lose 4 electrons — which requires too much energy. So it prefers to share 4 electrons → forms 4 covalent bonds. This is easier and more stable.
vii.
What is Snell's law of refraction? Write its mathematical form.
Snell's Law: The ratio of sine of angle of incidence to sine of angle of refraction is constant for a given pair of media.
n = sin i / sin r = v₁/v₂ = n₂/n₁
Where n = refractive index, i = angle of incidence, r = angle of refraction, v₁ and v₂ = speeds in respective media.

a.
Explain the types of chemical reactions with one example each: (i) Combination (ii) Decomposition (iii) Displacement (iv) Double Displacement
(i) Combination: Two or more substances combine to form a single product.
CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂
(ii) Decomposition: A single substance breaks down into two or more simpler substances.
2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂ (electrolysis)
(iii) Displacement: More reactive element displaces less reactive from its compound.
Fe + CuSO₄ → FeSO₄ + Cu
(iv) Double Displacement: Exchange of ions between two compounds; often forms precipitate.
AgNO₃ + NaCl → AgCl↓ + NaNO₃
b.
What is an acid? What is a base? Give two properties of each. What is neutralisation?
Acid: A substance that gives H⁺ ions in water. Tastes sour; turns blue litmus red; pH < 7.
Properties: (1) Reacts with metals to give H₂ gas: Zn + H₂SO₄ → ZnSO₄ + H₂↑ (2) Reacts with carbonates: Na₂CO₃ + 2HCl → 2NaCl + H₂O + CO₂

Base: A substance that gives OH⁻ ions in water. Tastes bitter; turns red litmus blue; pH > 7.
Properties: (1) Reacts with acids (neutralisation) (2) Soapy/slippery to touch

Neutralisation: Reaction between an acid and a base to form salt and water.
HCl + NaOH → NaCl + H₂O
c.
Explain the working of an electric motor with a neat labelled diagram.
Electric Motor: Converts electrical energy → mechanical (rotational) energy.

Principle: A current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field experiences a force (Fleming's Left Hand Rule).

Construction: Rectangular coil ABCD placed between poles of a magnet; connected to battery through split-ring commutator and carbon brushes.

Working:
1. Current flows through coil via brushes.
2. Arm AB experiences upward force; arm CD experiences downward force (by FLHR).
3. Coil rotates. After half rotation, commutator reverses current direction.
4. Forces reverse → coil continues rotating in same direction.

Use: Fans, pumps, mixers, electric vehicles.

What is electromagnetic induction? State Faraday's laws of electromagnetic induction.
Electromagnetic Induction: The phenomenon of producing an induced EMF (and hence current) in a coil/conductor due to change in magnetic flux is called electromagnetic induction. Discovered by Michael Faraday (1831).

Faraday's Laws:

Application: Electric generator — converts mechanical energy to electrical energy using electromagnetic induction.

Explain refraction through a glass slab. What is meant by lateral displacement?

d = t × sin(i − r) / cos r
where t = thickness of slab, i = angle of incidence, r = angle of refraction.
Increases with: greater angle of incidence, thicker slab, higher refractive index.
