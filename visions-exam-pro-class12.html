🔑 Must Know Reactions
  • Dehydration of alcohol: ROH → alkene + H₂O (conc. H₂SO₄, 170°C)
  • Oxidation: 1° alcohol → aldehyde → acid; 2° → ketone; 3° → no oxidation
  • Kolbe-Schmitt: Sodium phenoxide + CO₂ → sodium salicylate → salicylic acid
  • Reimer-Tiemann: Phenol + CHCl₃ + KOH → salicylaldehyde
  • Williamson synthesis: R−ONa + R'X → R−O−R' (ether)
  • Grignard + Aldehyde: R−MgX + RCHO → 2° alcohol; + Ketone → 3° alcohol

🔑 Must Know
  • Law of segregation: alleles separate during gamete formation
  • Law of independent assortment: genes on different chromosomes assort independently
  • Monohybrid ratio: 3:1 (phenotypic); 1:2:1 (genotypic)
  • Dihybrid ratio: 9:3:3:1 (phenotypic)
  • Incomplete dominance: 1:2:1 (e.g., red × white → pink)
  • ABO blood groups: codominance; I^A I^B = AB (universal recipient); ii = O
  • Sex determination: XX (female); XY (male); sperm determines sex

Chapter 4 · Biology
Molecular Basis of Inheritance (DNA) — One Shot
⭐ Every Year
🔑 Must Know
  • DNA structure: double helix; antiparallel strands; A-T, G-C base pairs; Chargaff's rule
  • Replication: semi-conservative; DNA polymerase; leading & lagging strand
  • Transcription: DNA → mRNA; RNA polymerase; template strand
  • Translation: mRNA → Protein; ribosome; tRNA anticodon; codon = 3 bases
  • Genetic code: 64 codons; 61 sense + 3 stop (UAA, UAG, UGA)
  • AUG: start codon (Methionine); universal genetic code

Chapter 7 · Biology
Plant Physiology — Photosynthesis & Respiration — One Shot
⭐ High Weightage
🔑 Must Know
  • Photosynthesis: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + light → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂
  • Light reaction: in thylakoid; produces ATP, NADPH, O₂
  • Dark reaction (Calvin cycle): in stroma; uses CO₂; produces glucose
  • C3 plants: first product = 3-PGA (3 carbon); wheat, rice, oats
  • C4 plants: first product = OAA (4 carbon); sugarcane, maize
  • Cellular respiration: Glycolysis (cytoplasm) → Krebs cycle (mitochondria)
  • ATP yield: aerobic = 36–38 ATP; anaerobic = 2 ATP

Differentiation & Applications — One Shot
⭐ Every Year
🔑 Must Know
  • d/dx(xⁿ) = nxⁿ⁻¹; d/dx(eˣ) = eˣ; d/dx(ln x) = 1/x
  • Chain rule: dy/dx = (dy/du)·(du/dx)
  • Product rule: d(uv)/dx = u·dv/dx + v·du/dx

  • Increasing: f'(x) > 0; Decreasing: f'(x) < 0
  • Tangent slope: m = dy/dx at given point
