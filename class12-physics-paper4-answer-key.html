SECTION A — MCQ Answers 10 × 1 = 10
(i) Wheatstone bridge — balanced condition
Answer: (A) P/Q = R/S
Balanced Wheatstone bridge: P/Q = R/S → no current through galvanometer
(ii) Velocity of light in medium of refractive index n
Answer: (B) c/n
n = c/v → v = c/n where c = 3×10⁸ m/s
(iii) Nuclear force is
Answer: (C) short range, very strong, charge independent
Nuclear force: acts only within ~10⁻¹⁵ m (femtometre range), strongest fundamental force, same between p-p, p-n, n-n
(iv) Photoelectric current depends on
Answer: (A) intensity of incident light
More photons = more electrons ejected = higher current. Frequency affects KE, not number of electrons.
(v) Unit of magnetic field B
Answer: (B) Tesla
1 Tesla = 1 Wb/m² = 1 N/(A·m)   SI unit of magnetic flux density is Tesla (T)
(vi) For total internal reflection, light must travel from
Answer: (A) denser to rarer medium
TIR occurs: (1) denser → rarer medium AND (2) angle of incidence > critical angle
(vii) Zener diode operates in
Answer: (B) reverse bias breakdown region
Zener diode is specially designed to operate in reverse breakdown → maintains constant voltage V_Z
(viii) Condition for constructive interference
Answer: (A) path difference = nλ (n = 0,1,2...)
Constructive: Δ = nλ → bright fringe  |  Destructive: Δ = (2n−1)λ/2 → dark fringe
(ix) Velocity selector selects particles with
Answer: (C) v = E/B
When electric force = magnetic force: qE = qvB → v = E/B (velocity selector)
(x) In OR gate, output is 0 only when
Answer: (D) both inputs are 0
OR gate: Y = A + B  |  Output is 0 ONLY when A = 0 AND B = 0
SECTION B — Very Short Answers 8 × 1 = 8
(i) Define electric potential
Electric potential at a point = work done per unit positive charge in bringing a unit positive charge from infinity to that point without acceleration. V = W/q   Unit: Volt (V) = J/C
(ii) State Kirchhoff's voltage law (KVL)
The algebraic sum of all EMFs and potential drops in any closed loop of a circuit is zero. ΣV = 0   (Conservation of energy)
(iii) What is magnetic declination?
Magnetic declination is the angle between geographic north and magnetic north at a given location on Earth's surface.
(iv) Define rms value of AC
RMS (root mean square) value = the equivalent DC value that produces the same heating effect. V_rms = V₀/√2   I_rms = I₀/√2
(v) What is polarisation of light?
Polarisation is the process of restricting the vibrations of light waves to a single plane (plane of vibration ⊥ direction of propagation). Only transverse waves can be polarised (light can be, sound cannot).
(vi) Write Einstein's mass-energy relation
E = mc² m = mass, c = speed of light = 3×10⁸ m/s  |  1 amu = 931.5 MeV
(vii) What is a NAND gate?
NAND = NOT + AND. Output is LOW (0) only when ALL inputs are HIGH (1). Y = A̅·B̅   NAND is a universal gate.
(viii) Define specific resistance
Specific resistance (ρ) = resistance of a conductor of unit length and unit cross-sectional area. R = ρL/A → ρ = RA/L   Unit: Ω·m
SECTION C — Short Answers (selected) 8 × 2 = 16
(i) Series combination of capacitors
In series: same charge Q on each capacitor, voltages add up. V = V₁ + V₂ + V₃ → Q/C_eq = Q/C₁ + Q/C₂ + Q/C₃ 1/C_eq = 1/C₁ + 1/C₂ + 1/C₃
(iii) Full-wave rectifier
Uses two diodes with a centre-tap transformer (or 4 diodes in bridge). During +ve half cycle: D₁ conducts → current through R_L in one direction During −ve half cycle: D₂ conducts → current through R_L in SAME direction Output: pulsating DC with twice the frequency of input AC
(v) Brewster's law
When light is incident at Brewster's angle θ_B, the reflected light is completely plane-polarised. tan θ_B = n   (Brewster's law) where n = refractive index of medium At θ_B, reflected and refracted rays are perpendicular: θ_B + θ_r = 90°
SECTION D — Medium Answers (selected) 6 × 3 = 18
(i) Force between two parallel conductors
Wire 1 carries I₁ → creates B₁ = μ₀I₁/2πd at location of wire 2 Force on wire 2: F = I₂lB₁ = μ₀I₁I₂l/2πd F/l = μ₀I₁I₂/2πd Parallel currents attract; antiparallel currents repel. 1 Ampere is defined using this.
(iii) Radioactive decay law
Rate of disintegration ∝ number of atoms present: dN/dt = −λN Solving: N = N₀e^(−λt) N = N₀e^(−λt)   where λ = decay constant Half-life: T₁/₂ = 0.693/λ  |  Mean life: τ = 1/λ
SECTION E — Long Answers (selected) 4 × 4 = 16
(i) Electric potential — derivation
Definition: V = W/q₀ (work done per unit positive charge) For point charge Q: V = kQ/r (using work-energy theorem) Relation between E and V: E = −dV/dr Equipotential surface: surface where V = constant → E ⊥ surface For a system of charges: V_total = kq₁/r₁ + kq₂/r₂ + ... (superposition)
(iv) Diffraction at single slit
Condition for minima: a sinθ = nλ (n = ±1, ±2...) Central maximum: widest and brightest (θ = 0) Width of central max: 2λ/a (angular) → 2λD/a (on screen) Intensity pattern: I = I₀ (sinα/α)² where α = (πa sinθ)/λ Secondary maxima occur at asinθ = (2n+1)λ/2   Intensity decreases rapidly
