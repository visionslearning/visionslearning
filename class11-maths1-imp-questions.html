Chapter 1 — Angle and its Measurement

Degrees, Radians, Arc Length, Area of Sector

Q 1 Convert the following angles. ⭐ Most IMP Degree ↔ Radian
(i) Convert 240° into radians
Given Angle = 240°
Formula Radians = Degrees × π/180
Step 1 = 240 × π/180
Step 2 = 4π/3
∴ Ans 240° = 4π/3 radians
(ii) Convert 570° into radians
Given Angle = 570°
Step 1 = 570 × π/180
Step 2 = 19π/6
∴ Ans 570° = 19π/6 radians
(iii) Convert 5π/3 radians into degrees
Given Angle = 5π/3 radians
Formula Degrees = Radians × 180/π
Step 1 = (5π/3) × (180/π)
Step 2 = 5 × 60 = 300°
∴ Ans 5π/3 radians = 300°
(iv) Convert −11π/18 radians into degrees
Step 1 = (−11π/18) × (180/π)
Step 2 = −11 × 10 = −110°
∴ Ans −11π/18 = −110°
Q 2 Arc Length and Area of Sector. ⭐ Most IMP Arc & Sector
(i) Circle radius = 6 cm, arc length = 5π cm. Find angle at centre.
Given r = 6 cm,  l = 5π cm
To Find Angle θ at centre (in degrees)
Formula l = rθ  (θ in radians)
Step 1 θ = l / r = 5π / 6 radians
Step 2 = (5π/6) × (180/π) = 150°
∴ Ans θ = 150°
(ii) r = 7 cm, θ = 60°. Find area of sector.
Given r = 7 cm,  θ = 60° = π/3 rad
Formula Area = ½ r²θ  (θ in radians)
Step 1 = ½ × 7² × π/3
Step 2 = ½ × 49 × π/3
Step 3 = 49π/6
∴ Ans Area = 49π/6 cm²
(iii) r = 12 cm, θ = 120°. Find arc length and area of sector.
Given r = 12 cm,  θ = 120° = 2π/3 rad
Arc l = rθ = 12 × 2π/3 = 8π cm
Area = ½r²θ = ½ × 144 × 2π/3 = 48π cm²
∴ Ans Arc = 8π cm  |  Area = 48π cm²

Chapter 2 — Trigonometry - I

Trig Ratios, Identities, Allied Angles

Q 4 sin θ = 3/5, θ in 2nd quadrant. Find all other trig ratios. ⭐ Most IMP Trig Ratios
Given sin θ = 3/5,  θ ∈ 2nd quadrant
To Find cos θ, tan θ, cot θ, sec θ, cosec θ
Identity sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
Step 1 cos²θ = 1 − sin²θ = 1 − 9/25 = 16/25
Step 2 cos θ = ±4/5
In 2nd quadrant cos is negative
∴ cos θ = −4/5
Step 3 tan θ = sin θ / cos θ = (3/5) / (−4/5) = −3/4
Step 4 cot θ = 1/tan θ = −4/3
sec θ = 1/cos θ = −5/4
cosec θ = 1/sin θ = 5/3
∴ Ans cos θ = −4/5
tan θ = −3/4
cot θ = −4/3
sec θ = −5/4
cosec θ = 5/3
Q 5 Prove the following identities. ⭐ Most IMP Trig Identities
(i) Prove: (1 − sin²θ) sec²θ = 1
LHS = (1 − sin²θ) sec²θ
Step 1 = cos²θ × sec²θ    [∵ 1 − sin²θ = cos²θ]
Step 2 = cos²θ × (1/cos²θ)    [∵ sec θ = 1/cos θ]
Step 3 = 1 = RHS
∴ Proved LHS = RHS ✓
(ii) Prove: tan θ + cot θ = sec θ · cosec θ
LHS = tan θ + cot θ
Step 1 = sin θ/cos θ + cos θ/sin θ
Step 2 = (sin²θ + cos²θ) / (cos θ · sin θ)
Step 3 = 1 / (cos θ · sin θ)    [∵ sin²θ + cos²θ = 1]
Step 4 = sec θ · cosec θ = RHS
∴ Proved LHS = RHS ✓
(iii) Prove: (sec θ − tan θ)² = (1 − sin θ)/(1 + sin θ)
LHS = (sec θ − tan θ)²
Step 1 = (1/cos θ − sin θ/cos θ)²
Step 2 = ((1 − sin θ)/cos θ)²
Step 3 = (1 − sin θ)² / cos²θ
Step 4 = (1 − sin θ)² / (1 − sin²θ)    [∵ cos²θ = 1 − sin²θ]
Step 5 = (1 − sin θ)² / [(1 − sin θ)(1 + sin θ)]
Step 6 = (1 − sin θ) / (1 + sin θ) = RHS
∴ Proved LHS = RHS ✓
Q 6 Evaluate using allied angles. ⭐ IMP Allied Angles
(i) sin 150° + cos 300° − tan 135°
sin 150° = sin(180°−30°) = sin 30° = 1/2
cos 300° = cos(360°−60°) = cos 60° = 1/2
tan 135° = tan(180°−45°) = −tan 45° = −1
Sum = 1/2 + 1/2 − (−1) = 1 + 1 = 2
∴ Ans = 2

Chapter 3 — Trigonometry - II

Sum formulae, Multiple angles, sin 3A, sin 75°

Q 7 Prove: sin(A+B)·sin(A−B) = sin²A − sin²B. ⭐ Most IMP Sum Formulae
Formulae sin(A+B) = sin A cos B + cos A sin B
sin(A−B) = sin A cos B − cos A sin B
LHS = sin(A+B) · sin(A−B)
Step 1 = (sin A cos B + cos A sin B)(sin A cos B − cos A sin B)
Step 2 = (sin A cos B)² − (cos A sin B)²    [∵ (a+b)(a−b) = a²−b²]
Step 3 = sin²A cos²B − cos²A sin²B
Step 4 = sin²A(1−sin²B) − (1−sin²A)sin²B
Step 5 = sin²A − sin²A sin²B − sin²B + sin²A sin²B
Step 6 = sin²A − sin²B = RHS
∴ Proved LHS = RHS ✓
Q 8 Multiple angle formulae. ⭐ Most IMP Multiple Angles
(i) sin A = 3/5, A in 1st quadrant. Find sin 2A, cos 2A, tan 2A.
Given sin A = 3/5,  cos A = 4/5 (1st quadrant)
sin 2A = 2 sin A cos A = 2 × (3/5) × (4/5) = 24/25
cos 2A = 1 − 2sin²A = 1 − 2(9/25) = 1 − 18/25 = 7/25
tan 2A = sin 2A / cos 2A = (24/25) / (7/25) = 24/7
∴ Ans sin 2A = 24/25  |  cos 2A = 7/25  |  tan 2A = 24/7
(ii) Prove: sin 3A = 3 sin A − 4 sin³A
LHS = sin 3A = sin(2A + A)
Step 1 = sin 2A cos A + cos 2A sin A
Step 2 = (2 sin A cos A) cos A + (1 − 2sin²A) sin A
Step 3 = 2 sin A cos²A + sin A − 2sin³A
Step 4 = 2 sin A(1 − sin²A) + sin A − 2sin³A
Step 5 = 2 sin A − 2sin³A + sin A − 2sin³A
Step 6 = 3 sin A − 4sin³A = RHS
∴ Proved LHS = RHS ✓
(iii) Find sin 75° and cos 75° using compound angle formula.
Given 75° = 45° + 30°
sin 75° = sin(45°+30°) = sin45°cos30° + cos45°sin30°
Step = (1/√2)(√3/2) + (1/√2)(1/2) = (√3+1)/(2√2)
sin 75° (√6 + √2) / 4
cos 75° = cos(45°+30°) = cos45°cos30° − sin45°sin30°
Step = (1/√2)(√3/2) − (1/√2)(1/2) = (√3−1)/(2√2)
cos 75° (√6 − √2) / 4
Q 9 Prove: sin 20° sin 40° sin 80° = √3/8. ⭐ IMP Product Formula
Formula sin A · sin(60°−A) · sin(60°+A) = ¼ sin 3A
Step 1 sin 20° · sin 40° · sin 80°
= sin 20° · sin(60°−20°) · sin(60°+20°)
Step 2 = ¼ sin(3×20°) = ¼ sin 60°
Step 3 = ¼ × (√3/2) = √3/8
∴ Proved sin 20° sin 40° sin 80° = √3/8 ✓

Chapter 4 — Determinants and Matrices

Determinant value, Matrix operations, Inverse, Cramer's Rule

Q 10 Evaluate the determinants. ⭐ Most IMP Determinants
(i) |3 −1; 4 2|
Formula |a b; c d| = ad − bc
Step 1 = (3)(2) − (−1)(4)
Step 2 = 6 + 4 = 10
∴ D = 10
(ii) |1 2 3; 2 −1 1; 3 1 −2| — expand along R₁
Step 1 D = 1·|−1 1; 1 −2| − 2·|2 1; 3 −2| + 3·|2 −1; 3 1|
Step 2 = 1·[(−1)(−2)−(1)(1)] − 2·[(2)(−2)−(1)(3)] + 3·[(2)(1)−(−1)(3)]
Step 3 = 1·[2−1] − 2·[−4−3] + 3·[2+3]
Step 4 = 1(1) − 2(−7) + 3(5)
Step 5 = 1 + 14 + 15 = 30
∴ D = 30
Q 11 Matrix Operations — Addition, Multiplication, Inverse. ⭐ Most IMP Matrices
(i) A = [1 2; 3 4], B = [5 6; 7 8]. Find A+B, A−B, 2A.
A + B = [1+5 2+6; 3+7 4+8] = [6 8; 10 12]
A − B = [1−5 2−6; 3−7 4−8] = [−4 −4; −4 −4]
2A = [2 4; 6 8]
(ii) Find the inverse of A = [2 1; 5 3]
Formula A⁻¹ = (1/det A) × adj A
det A = (2)(3) − (1)(5) = 6 − 5 = 1
adj A = [3 −1; −5 2]
A⁻¹ = (1/1) × [3 −1; −5 2]
∴ A⁻¹ = [3 −1; −5 2]
Check A × A⁻¹ = [2 1; 5 3][3 −1; −5 2] = [6−5 −2+2; 15−15 −5+6] = [1 0; 0 1] = I ✓
Q 12 Solve using Cramer's Rule. ⭐ Most IMP Cramer's Rule
(i) 3x − y = 5    2x + 3y = 12
Given 3x − y = 5  …(1)
2x + 3y = 12  …(2)
Formula x = Dx/D    y = Dy/D
Find D D = |3 −1; 2 3|
= (3)(3) − (−1)(2)
= 9 + 2
= 11
Find Dx Dx = |5 −1; 12 3|
= (5)(3) − (−1)(12)
= 15 + 12
= 27
Find Dy Dy = |3 5; 2 12|
= (3)(12) − (5)(2)
= 36 − 10
= 26
x = Dx/D = 27/11
y = Dy/D = 26/11
∴ Ans x = 27/11    y = 26/11
(ii) 2x + 3y + 3z = 5    x − 2y + z = −4    3x − y − 2z = 3
Find D D = |2 3 3; 1 −2 1; 3 −1 −2|
= 2[(−2)(−2)−(1)(−1)] − 3[(1)(−2)−(1)(3)] + 3[(1)(−1)−(−2)(3)]
= 2[4+1] − 3[−2−3] + 3[−1+6]
= 2(5) − 3(−5) + 3(5)
= 10 + 15 + 15
= 40
Find Dx Dx = |5 3 3; −4 −2 1; 3 −1 −2|
= 5[4+1] − 3[8−3] + 3[4+6]
= 25 − 15 + 30
= 40
x = Dx/D = 40/40 = 1
Find Dy Dy = |2 5 3; 1 −4 1; 3 3 −2|
= 2(8−3) − 5(−2−3) + 3(3+12)
= 10 + 25 + 45
= 80
y = Dy/D = 80/40 = 2
Find Dz Dz = |2 3 5; 1 −2 −4; 3 −1 3|
= 2(−6−4) − 3(3+12) + 5(−1+6)
= −20 − 45 + 25
= −40
z = Dz/D = −40/40 = −1
∴ Ans x = 1    y = 2    z = −1
Check 2(1)+3(2)+3(−1) = 2+6−3 = 5 ✓  |  1−4+(−1) = −4 ✓
