SECTION B — Very Short Answers 8 × 1 = 8
(i) Define dielectric constant
Dielectric constant (K) = ratio of capacitance with dielectric to capacitance without dielectric. K = C/C₀ = ε/ε₀   K ≥ 1 always. Also called relative permittivity.
(ii) State the right-hand thumb rule
If a straight conductor is held in the right hand with the thumb pointing in the direction of current, the curled fingers give the direction of circular magnetic field lines around the conductor.
(iii) What is impedance of an AC circuit?
Impedance (Z) is the total opposition to flow of AC current in a circuit. Z = √(R² + (X_L − X_C)²)   Unit: Ohm (Ω)
(iv) Define wavefront
A wavefront is a surface connecting all points which are in the same phase of vibration (same path difference from source). Types: Spherical (point source), Cylindrical (line source), Plane (very distant source)
(v) What is Compton effect?
When X-rays are scattered by free electrons, the scattered X-ray has a longer wavelength than the incident one. Δλ = (h/m_e c)(1 − cosθ)   Compton shift formula
(vi) Define atomic number and mass number
Atomic number (Z): Number of protons in the nucleus. Mass number (A): Total number of nucleons (protons + neutrons) in the nucleus. A = Z + N
(vii) State the function of an AND gate
AND gate output is HIGH (1) only when ALL inputs are HIGH (1). Y = A·B   Truth table: 00→0, 01→0, 10→0, 11→1
(viii) What is nuclear fission?
Nuclear fission is the splitting of a heavy nucleus (like ²³⁵U) into two lighter nuclei on bombardment with slow neutrons, releasing large amounts of energy. ²³⁵₉₂U + ¹₀n → ¹⁴¹₅₆Ba + ⁹²₃₆Kr + 3¹₀n + energy
SECTION C — Short Answers (selected) 8 × 2 = 16
(i) Potential energy of a dipole in electric field
Work done to rotate dipole from θ₁ to θ₂: W = pE(cosθ₁ − cosθ₂) Potential energy: U = −pE cosθ = −p⃗·E⃗ Stable equilibrium: θ = 0° → U_min = −pE Unstable equilibrium: θ = 180° → U_max = +pE
(iii) Transformer principle
Principle: Mutual induction — changing flux in primary coil induces EMF in secondary coil. V_s/V_p = N_s/N_p = I_p/I_s Step-up: N_s > N_p → V_s > V_p  |  Step-down: N_s < N_p → V_s < V_p
SECTION D — Medium Answers (selected) 6 × 3 = 18
(ii) Lens formula derivation
Using sign convention and similar triangles for a thin convex lens: For real image: PO/PI = f/(v−f) and also PO/PI = u/v ... (geometry) Combining: f/(v−f) = f/(u−f) ... leads to: 1/v − 1/u = 1/f   (Lens formula)
(iv) Magnetic field on axis of solenoid
A solenoid with n turns per metre, carrying current I: B = μ₀nI (inside solenoid)   B = μ₀nI This is derived using Ampere's circuital law with rectangular Amperian loop.

(i) Moving coil galvanometer
Construction: A rectangular coil of N turns, suspended between pole pieces of a permanent magnet. Soft iron core makes B radial. Working: Current I flows → torque τ = NBIA (deflecting) Restoring torque by spring: τ_r = kθ At equilibrium: NBIA = kθ θ = (NBA/k) × I   → deflection ∝ current Current sensitivity = NBA/k
(v) Period of satellite T = 2π√(r³/GM)
Derivation: Centripetal force = Gravitational force mv²/r = GMm/r² v² = GM/r → v = √(GM/r) Time period: T = 2πr/v = 2πr/√(GM/r) = 2π√(r³/GM) T = 2π√(r³/GM) Geostationary satellite: T = 24 hours, r ≈ 42,400 km, orbits above equator → appears stationary → used for TV/communication/weather
