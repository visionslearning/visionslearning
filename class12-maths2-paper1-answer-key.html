SECTION A — MCQ Answers 8 × 1 = 8
(i) P(A∩B) when P(A)=1/4, P(B)=1/3, independent
Answer: (B) 1/12
For independent events: P(A∩B) = P(A)×P(B) = 1/4 × 1/3 = 1/12
(ii) Variance of B(n,p)
Answer: (C) npq
For Binomial distribution: Mean = np, Variance = npq where q = 1−p
(iii) Regression coefficient b_yx in y = 2x + 5
Answer: (A) 2
Regression line y on x: y = b_yx·x + a → b_yx = slope = 2
(iv) Optimal solution in LPP
Answer: (B) a corner point of feasible region
By the Fundamental Theorem of LPP: optimal solution occurs at a vertex (corner point) of the feasible region.
(v) Seasonal variation
Answer: (C) repeats within a year
Seasonal variation: regular, periodic fluctuations within a year (e.g., sales of ice cream high in summer)
(vi) Paasche's price index uses
Answer: (B) current year quantities
Laspeyre's → base year quantities (q₀)  |  Paasche's → current year quantities (q₁)
(vii) Standard normal distribution — mean
Answer: (B) 0
Standard normal: N(0,1) → mean = 0, variance = 1, SD = 1
(viii) Poisson distribution — mean
Answer: (C) λ
For Poisson distribution: Mean = λ, Variance = λ (both equal)   P(X=r) = e^(−λ)·λʳ/r!
