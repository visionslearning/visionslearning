Q.4 (i)
State and prove addition theorem. Find P(sum=8 OR div by 3) for two dice.
Theorem
P(A∪B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A∩B)
Proof: A∪B = A ∪ (B−A). P(A∪B) = P(A)+P(B−A) = P(A)+P(B)−P(A∩B) ✓
Numerical Sum=8: (2,6),(3,5),(4,4),(5,3),(6,2) = 5 outcomes Div by 3 (sum=3,6,9,12): 12 outcomes Both (sum=12, div by 3): (6,6) = 1 P = 5/36 + 12/36 − 1/36 = 16/36 = 4/9
Q.4 (ii)
Bayes' theorem. Red ball from 3 bags.
Bayes TheoremP(Aᵢ|B) = P(B|Aᵢ)P(Aᵢ) / ΣP(B|Aⱼ)P(Aⱼ)
Numerical Bag A: 2R,3B | Bag B: 4R,1B | Bag C: 3R,2B | P(each)=1/3 P(R) = (1/3)(2/5)+(1/3)(4/5)+(1/3)(3/5) = (2+4+3)/15 = 3/5 P(A|R) = (2/5×1/3)/(3/5) = (2/15)/(3/5) = 2/9
