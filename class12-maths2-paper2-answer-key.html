Q.1 (i)
P(A∪B) when A, B mutually exclusive, P(A)=0.3, P(B)=0.4:
✔ Answer: (B) 0.7
Explanation Mutually exclusive: P(A∩B) = 0 P(A∪B) = P(A) + P(B) = 0.3 + 0.4 = 0.7
Q.1 (ii)
B(n,p) with mean = 4 and variance = 2, then p =
✔ Answer: (C) 1/2
Solution Mean = np = 4  |  Variance = npq = 2 Variance/Mean = q = 2/4 = 1/2 → p = 1 − q = 1/2
Q.1 (iii)
The value of r lies between:
✔ Answer: (B) −1 and 1
Explanation Pearson's r ∈ [−1, 1] always. r = +1: perfect positive correlation; r = −1: perfect negative; r = 0: no linear correlation
Q.1 (iv)
LPP feasible region is the set of:
✔ Answer: (C) feasible solutions
Explanation Feasible region = set of all points that satisfy ALL constraints simultaneously (including non-negativity conditions) Each point in the feasible region is a feasible solution
Q.1 (v)
Which component of time series repeats cyclically?
✔ Answer: (D) Cyclical
Explanation Trend (T): long-term direction over years Seasonal (S): regular, periodic within a year (quarterly, monthly) Cyclical (C): wave-like repetition over several years (business cycles, trade cycles) Irregular (I): unpredictable random fluctuations
Q.1 (vi)
Paasche's index uses:
✔ Answer: (B) current year weights (q₁)
Explanation Paasche's: P = (Σp₁q₁/Σp₀q₁) × 100 (current year quantities q₁) Laspeyre's: L = (Σp₁q₀/Σp₀q₀) × 100 (base year quantities q₀)
Q.1 (vii)
For standard normal distribution, the mean is:
✔ Answer: (B) 0
Explanation Standard normal N(0,1): mean = 0, variance = 1, SD = 1 Z = (X − μ)/σ   Standard normal variable, symmetric about 0
Q.1 (viii)
For Poisson distribution, variance equals:
✔ Answer: (D) λ (mean)
Explanation For Poisson distribution P(X=r) = e^(−λ)λʳ/r!: Mean = Variance = λ This equality of mean and variance is a key identifying property of Poisson distribution

Q.3 (x)
z-test: x̄=82, s=8, n=64, μ=80. Test at 5%.
Solution H₀: μ=80, H₁: μ≠80 (two-tailed) z = (x̄−μ)/(s/√n) = (82−80)/(8/8) = 2/1 = 2 |z| = 2 > 1.96 → Reject H₀
Reject H₀. Population mean ≠ 80 at 5% significance level.

State and prove the multiplication theorem of probability. Find P(drawing 3 aces).
StatementP(A∩B) = P(A) × P(B|A) = P(B) × P(A|B)
Proof P(B|A) = P(A∩B)/P(A) by definition of conditional probability → P(A∩B) = P(A) × P(B|A) ✓
Numerical P(1st ace) = 4/52, P(2nd ace|1st) = 3/51, P(3rd ace|1st,2nd) = 2/50 P(3 aces) = (4/52)(3/51)(2/50) = 24/132600
P(3 aces) = 24/132600 = 1/5525 ≈ 0.000181
