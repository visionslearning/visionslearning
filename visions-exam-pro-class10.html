Chemical Reactions & Equations
⭐ High Weightage
Types of reactions
Balancing equations
Oxidation & Reduction
Exothermic / Endothermic
Corrosion & Rancidity
Displacement reactions
🔑 Must Know
  • Combination: A + B → AB (CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂)
  • Decomposition: AB → A + B (2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂)
  • Displacement: Fe + CuSO₄ → FeSO₄ + Cu (more reactive displaces less)
  • Double displacement: AgNO₃ + NaCl → AgCl↓ + NaNO₃ (precipitate)
  • Oxidation: Loss of electrons / gain of oxygen
  • Reduction: Gain of electrons / loss of oxygen
  • Corrosion: Fe + O₂ + H₂O → Fe₂O₃·xH₂O (rust)
⚡ Board Exam Tip

Balance equations by hit-and-trial. Write state symbols (s), (l), (g), (aq). Identify type of reaction — 2 marks asked every year.

⭐ Every Year

Electric power
Heating effect

Joule's Law
🔑 Must Know
  • Ohm's Law: V = IR; R = V/I; unit of resistance = Ohm (Ω)
  • Series: R = R₁ + R₂ + R₃; same current; different voltage
  • Parallel: 1/R = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂; same voltage; different current
  • Power: P = VI = I²R = V²/R; unit = Watt (W)
  • Joule's Law: H = I²Rt (heating effect)
  • Resistivity: R = ρL/A; unit of ρ = Ω·m

Refraction of Light — Lenses & Human Eye
⭐ Every Year
Snell's Law
Convex & Concave lens
Lens formula
Power of lens
Defects of vision
Dispersion / Rainbow
🔑 Must Know
  • Snell's Law: n₁ sinθ₁ = n₂ sinθ₂; n = c/v
  • Lens formula: 1/f = 1/v − 1/u
  • Power of lens: P = 1/f (metres); unit = Dioptre (D)
  • Magnification: m = v/u = h'/h
  • Myopia (short sight): corrected by concave lens
  • Hypermetropia (long sight): corrected by convex lens
  • Dispersion: VIBGYOR — Violet bends most, Red bends least

Chapter 2 · Science 2
Life Processes — Nutrition, Respiration, Transport
⭐ Every Year
Photosynthesis
Respiration
Blood circulation
Excretion
Nephron / Kidney
Digestive system
🔑 Must Know
  • Photosynthesis: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + light → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ (chloroplast)
  • Aerobic respiration: C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + 38 ATP
  • Anaerobic (yeast): glucose → ethanol + CO₂
  • Anaerobic (muscle): glucose → lactic acid (cramps)
  • Heart: 4 chambers; double circulation; left = oxygenated, right = deoxygenated
  • Kidney/Nephron: Filtration → reabsorption → secretion → urine
⚡ Board Exam Tip

Draw labelled diagram of nephron and human heart — 3 marks each. Write equations for photosynthesis and respiration with conditions.

    Algebra · Class 10 Maths
    Quadratic Equations — One Shot
    ⭐ Every Year
    🔑 Must Know
    • Standard form: ax² + bx + c = 0 (a ≠ 0)
    • Quadratic formula: x = [−b ± √(b²−4ac)] / 2a
    • Discriminant (D): D = b²−4ac; D > 0: 2 real roots; D = 0: equal roots; D < 0: no real roots
    • Sum of roots: α + β = −b/a
    • Product of roots: αβ = c/a
    • Factorisation method: split middle term → group → factor

    Statistics · Class 10 Maths
    Mean, Median, Mode — One Shot
    ⭐ Every Year
    🔑 Must Know
    • Mean (direct): x̄ = Σfx / Σf
    • Median: L + [(n/2 − cf)/f] × h
    • Mode: L + [(f₁−f₀)/(2f₁−f₀−f₂)] × h
    • Relationship: Mode = 3 Median − 2 Mean
    • Always find cumulative frequency (cf) for median
    • Modal class = class with highest frequency

    Geometry · Class 10 Maths
    Triangles — Similarity & Pythagoras
    ⭐ High Weightage
  • AA criterion: Two triangles are similar if two angles are equal
  • SAS criterion: Two sides proportional + included angle equal
  • SSS criterion: All three sides proportional
  • Pythagoras theorem: (Hypotenuse)² = (Base)² + (Height)²
  • BPT (Basic Proportionality): If DE ∥ BC → AD/DB = AE/EC
  • Area ratio of similar triangles = (ratio of sides)²

    English · Class 10
    Grammar — One Shot (Tenses, Voice, Narration)
    ⭐ Every Year
  • Active to Passive: Object + is/are/was/were + V3 + by + Subject
  • Direct to Indirect: Change pronoun, tense, time words
  • Simple Present → Simple Past in indirect speech
  • Future tense: will/shall → would/should
  • Can → Could, May → Might, Must → Had to
  • Remove inverted commas; add "that/if/whether"
