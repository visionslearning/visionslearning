SECTION A — Q.1 : Select and write the correct answer[10 Marks]
(i)The moment of inertia of a thin uniform ring of mass M and radius R about its diameter is ______.[1]
(A) MR²(B) MR²/2(C) MR²/4(D) 2MR²
(ii)When a ballet dancer pulls in her arms while spinning, her angular velocity ______.[1]
(A) decreases(B) increases(C) remains same(D) becomes zero
(iii)The difference in tension at the bottom (TB) and top (TA) of a vertical circle is ______.[1]
(A) 2mg(B) 4mg(C) 6mg(D) 3mg
(iv)For a solid sphere rolling without slipping, the ratio of rotational KE to total KE is ______.[1]
(A) 5/7(B) 1/5(C) 2/7(D) 3/7
(v)The banking angle for a curved road is given by tan θ = ______.[1]
(A) v²rg(B) v²/rg(C) rg/v²(D) v/rg
(vi)In Young's double slit experiment, the fringe width β is given by ______.[1]
(A) λd/D(B) λD/d(C) dD/λ(D) λ/dD
(vii)According to Malus' law, intensity of transmitted polarised light I = ______.[1]
(A) I₀ sin²θ(B) I₀ cos²θ(C) I₀ tanθ(D) I₀/cos²θ
(viii)The condition for constructive interference (bright fringe) is that the path difference = ______.[1]
(A) nλ(B) (2n−1)λ/2(C) (2n+1)λ(D) nλ/2
(ix)Brewster's law states that the angle of polarisation iB and refractive index n are related as ______.[1]
(A) sin iB = n(B) cos iB = n(C) tan iB = n(D) cot iB = n
(x)In single slit diffraction, condition for the first minimum is ______. (a = slit width)[1]
(A) a sinθ = λ(B) a sinθ = λ/2(C) a sinθ = 2λ(D) a cosθ = λ
