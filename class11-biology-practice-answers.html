Biology — Chapter 2
Systematics of Living Organisms
Taxonomy · Classification · Nomenclature · Five Kingdom System
📌 Topics Covered
Systematics — branch of biology dealing with classification and naming
Taxonomy — identification, nomenclature, and classification of organisms
Nomenclature — Binomial nomenclature (Carolus Linnaeus)
Taxonomic Hierarchy — Kingdom → Phylum → Class → Order → Family → Genus → Species
Five Kingdom Classification — Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae, Animalia (R. H. Whittaker)
Virus — non-cellular; DNA or RNA; obligate intracellular parasite ★
Bacteria — prokaryotic; useful (Lactobacillus, Rhizobium) and harmful (TB, Cholera)
Fungi — heterotrophic; cell wall of chitin; saprophytic/parasitic/symbiotic

Biology — Chapter 4
Kingdom Animalia
Phyla of Animals · Porifera to Chordata · Classification Criteria
📌 Topics Covered
Classification criteria — levels of organisation, symmetry, coelom, segmentation, notochord
Porifera — sponges; canal system; spicules; e.g. Sycon, Spongilla ★
Coelenterata — cnidoblasts; polymorphism; e.g. Hydra, Jellyfish
Platyhelminthes — flat worms; triploblastic; e.g. Planaria, Tapeworm
Nematoda — round worms; pseudocoelom; e.g. Ascaris, Wuchereria
Annelida — segmented worms; true coelom; e.g. Earthworm, Leech
Arthropoda — largest phylum; jointed appendages; e.g. Cockroach, Prawn
Mollusca — mantle; radula; e.g. Pila, Octopus, Sepia
Echinodermata — water vascular system; e.g. Starfish, Sea urchin
Chordata — notochord; dorsal nerve cord; pharyngeal gill slits

Biology — Chapter 5
Cell Structure & Organization
Cell Theory · Prokaryotic vs Eukaryotic · Plasma Membrane · Golgi · Mitochondria · Nucleus · 9+2 Axoneme
📌 Topics Covered
Cell Basics & Microscopy — Mycoplasma (0.3µm), Ostrich egg, Nerve cell; EM = 500,000×
Cell Theory — Schleiden (1838), Schwann (1839), Virchow (1855); 6 postulates ★
Totipotency & Stem Cells — embryonic cells; medical applications ★
Prokaryotic vs Eukaryotic — full comparison table (8 features) ★
Prokaryotic Cell Structure — glycocalyx, mesosomes, plasmids (F & R), Theta model ★
Fluid Mosaic Model — Singer & Nicholson 1972; intrinsic/extrinsic proteins ★★
ER (RER vs SER) — RER = protein synthesis; SER = lipids, detox, Ca²⁺
Golgi Complex — cis/trans face; cisternal maturation model; Post Office of cell
Lysosomes — 4 types; suicide bags; Tay-Sach's disease; acrosome ★★
Mitochondria — cristae; oxysomes (F₁ = ATP synthase); 70S ribosomes ★★
Plastids — Leucoplast / Chromoplast / Chloroplast (thylakoids, grana, stroma)
Nucleus — nucleopores; nucleolus = ribosome biogenesis; euchromatin vs heterochromatin ★
Cilia & Flagella — 9+2 axoneme ★★ | Centrioles — 9 triplet microtubules; cartwheel
Endosymbiont Theory — origin of mitochondria & chloroplast from engulfed prokaryotes ★★
