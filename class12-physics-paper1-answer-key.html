SECTION A — MCQ Answers 10 × 1 = 10
(i) SI unit of electric flux
Answer: (A) N·m²/C
Φ = E·A·cosθ → unit = (N/C)×m² = N·m²/C
(ii) Capacitor plate separation doubled
Answer: (B) becomes half
C = ε₀A/d → if d doubles, C = ε₀A/2d = C/2 → capacitance halves
(iii) SI unit of self-inductance
Answer: (C) Henry
e = −L(dI/dt) → L = e/(dI/dt) → unit = V·s/A = Henry (H)
(iv) Light travels from glass to air
Answer: (B) bends away from the normal
Going from denser (glass) to rarer (air) medium → bends away from normal (Snell's law: n₁sinθ₁ = n₂sinθ₂)
(v) Energy of a photon
Answer: (B) hν
E = hν (Planck's equation) where h = 6.63×10⁻³⁴ J·s, ν = frequency → E = hν
(vi) Fraction remaining after 40 years (t₁/₂ = 10 years)
Answer: (B) 1/16
n = 40/10 = 4 half-lives N/N₀ = (1/2)⁴ = 1/16
(vii) Stopping potential in photoelectric effect
Answer: (B) frequency of light
eV₀ = hν − φ → stopping potential depends on frequency, NOT intensity
(viii) β = 100, I_b = 20 μA → I_c
Answer: (B) 2 mA
I_c = β × I_b = 100 × 20×10⁻⁶ = 2×10⁻³ A = 2 mA
(ix) Slit separation halved in YDSE
Answer: (B) doubles
β = λD/d → if d is halved → β = λD/(d/2) = 2λD/d → fringe width doubles
(x) Lenz's law is consequence of
Answer: (B) energy
Lenz's law: induced current opposes change in flux → ensures energy is conserved → conservation of energy
SECTION B — Very Short Answers 8 × 1 = 8
(i) Define electric flux
Electric flux is the total number of electric field lines passing perpendicularly through a surface. Φ = E⃗·A⃗ = EA cosθ   Unit: N·m²/C
(ii) State Ohm's law
At constant temperature, the current (I) flowing through a conductor is directly proportional to the potential difference (V) across it. V = IR, where R = resistance (constant)
(iii) Define magnetic flux and its SI unit
Magnetic flux (Φ) = total magnetic field lines passing through a surface = B⃗·A⃗ = BA cosθ SI unit: Weber (Wb) = T·m²
(iv) Define power factor of AC circuit
Power factor = cosφ = R/Z = true power / apparent power For purely resistive circuit: cosφ = 1  |  For purely reactive: cosφ = 0
(v) What is total internal reflection (TIR)?
When light travels from a denser to rarer medium and the angle of incidence exceeds the critical angle, all light is reflected back — no refraction. This is called Total Internal Reflection.
(vi) Define work function of a metal
Work function (φ) is the minimum energy required to eject one electron from the surface of a metal. Unit: eV (electron volt)  |  φ = hν₀ where ν₀ = threshold frequency
(vii) What is a rectifier?
A rectifier is an electronic device (uses diode/s) that converts alternating current (AC) to direct current (DC). Types: Half-wave rectifier, Full-wave rectifier (bridge or centre-tap)
(viii) State De Morgan's second theorem
A̅·B̅ = Ā + B̄   (overline of A·B = overline A OR overline B) i.e., complement of (A AND B) = (complement of A) OR (complement of B)
(ix) Define nuclear binding energy
Nuclear binding energy is the energy required to completely separate all nucleons (protons and neutrons) of a nucleus from each other. BE = Δm × c² where Δm = mass defect
(x) What is critical angle?
Critical angle (C) is the angle of incidence in denser medium for which angle of refraction in rarer medium is exactly 90°. sin C = n₂/n₁ = 1/n (for air-glass interface)
(xi) What is a photon? State its properties
A photon is a quantum (packet) of electromagnetic radiation with zero rest mass. Properties: (1) E = hν  (2) p = h/λ  (3) rest mass = 0  (4) travels at speed c = 3×10⁸ m/s  (5) no charge
(xii) State Coulomb's law
The force between two point charges is directly proportional to product of charges and inversely proportional to square of distance between them. F = kq₁q₂/r² where k = 9×10⁹ N·m²/C²

(i) Energy stored in a capacitor
Consider charging a capacitor from 0 to Q. At charge q, potential V = q/C Work done: dW = V·dq = (q/C)dq Total work: W = ∫₀^Q (q/C)dq = Q²/2C U = Q²/2C = (1/2)CV² = (1/2)QV   Energy stored = ½CV²

(vii) Bohr orbit radius
Bohr's condition: mvr = nh/2π ... (1) Centripetal force = Coulomb force: mv²/r = ke²/r² → mv² = ke²/r ... (2) From (1): v = nh/2πmr → substituting in (2): r_n = n²h²/4π²mke² = n² × 0.529 Å r_n = 0.529n² Å

SECTION E — Long Answers (selected) 4 × 4 = 16
(ii) Lens maker's formula + image position
Lens Maker's Formula: 1/f = (n−1)[1/R₁ − 1/R₂] Derivation: Using refraction at two spherical surfaces, applying n₁/v − n₂/u = (n₁−n₂)/R twice: Surface 1: n/v' − 1/u = (n−1)/R₁ Surface 2: 1/v − n/v' = (1−n)/R₂ Adding: 1/v − 1/u = (n−1)[1/R₁ − 1/R₂] = 1/f Image: u = −30 cm, f = +20 cm (convex) 1/v = 1/f + 1/u = 1/20 + 1/(−30) = 3/60 − 2/60 = 1/60 v = +60 cm (real image, on other side)
(iv) Einstein's photoelectric equation
Einstein's Equation: hν = φ + KE_max KE_max = hν − φ = hν − hν₀ Terms: hν = energy of incident photon | φ = work function | KE_max = max KE of ejected electron Given: ν = 8×10¹⁴ Hz, φ = 3 eV, h = 6.63×10⁻³⁴ J·s hν = 6.63×10⁻³⁴ × 8×10¹⁴ = 53.04×10⁻²⁰ J 53.04×10⁻²⁰ / 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ = 3.315 eV KE_max = 3.315 − 3 = 0.315 eV
(iii) YDSE — find wavelength
Given: d = 0.3 mm = 3×10⁻⁴ m, D = 1.5 m, β = 3 mm = 3×10⁻³ m β = λD/d → λ = βd/D (3×10⁻³ × 3×10⁻⁴) / 1.5 9×10⁻⁷ / 1.5 λ = 6×10⁻⁷ m = 600 nm (orange-red light)
