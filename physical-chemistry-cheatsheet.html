📌 CH1: Polarisation & Debye

PT = Pe + Pa + Po
  • Pe — Electronic (all molecules, temp-independent)
  • Pa — Atomic/Ionic (temp-independent)
  • Po — Orientation (polar only, temp-dependent ↓ as T↑)
PT = (4/3)πNAα + μ²NA/3kT = A + B/T
💡 Plot PT vs 1/T → slope = μ²NA/3k → find μ
μ = √(3k × Slope / NA)

📌 CH2: Colligative Properties

Depend on number of solute particles, NOT on nature of solute

1. Vapour Pressure — Raoult's Law

p = p° × x₁
(p°− p)/p° = x₂ = mole fraction of solute
M₂ = (W₂ × M₁ × p°) / (W₁ × (p°− p))

2. Boiling Point Elevation

ΔTb = Kb × m
Kb = RT_b² × M₁ / (1000 × lv)

3. Freezing Point Depression

ΔTf = Kf × m
Kf = RT_f² × M₁ / (1000 × lf)
M₂ = (1000 × Kf × W₂) / (ΔTf × W₁)
💡 Camphor Kf = 37.7 K·kg·mol⁻¹ (Rast method)
Beckmann thermometer = adjustable range thermometer

4. Osmotic Pressure — Van't Hoff

πV = nRT  →  π = CRT
M = WRT / πV
💡 Reverse Osmosis: apply pressure > π on solution side → pure water obtained

Van't Hoff Factor (i) — Electrolytes

i = observed / calculated (no dissociation)
ΔTb = i·Kb·m  |  ΔTf = i·Kf·m  |  π = i·CRT
