Q 1 Classify alcohols as primary, secondary and tertiary with examples and structures. 🔴 5× 3 Marks
Years: 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023
Alcohols are classified based on the number of carbon atoms directly attached to the carbon bearing the –OH group:
🟢 Primary (1°)
H H | | H–C–C–OH | | H H
Ethanol (C₂H₅OH)
–OH on C bonded to 1 alkyl group
🔵 Secondary (2°)
H OH H | | | H–C–C–C–H | | | H H H
Propan-2-ol
–OH on C bonded to 2 alkyl groups
🔴 Tertiary (3°)
CH₃ | H₃C–C–OH | CH₃
2-methylpropan-2-ol
–OH on C bonded to 3 alkyl groups
Rule: 1° alcohol — RCHOH₂; 2° alcohol — R₂CHOH; 3° alcohol — R₃COH
Q 2 Write the Lucas test for alcohols. How does it distinguish 1°, 2°, 3° alcohols? 🔴 5× 3 Marks
Years: 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2024 — Appeared every year
Lucas Reagent: Anhydrous ZnCl₂ dissolved in concentrated HCl.
Reactions
3° Alcohol + Lucas reagent Room temp→ Immediate turbidity
2° Alcohol + Lucas reagent Room temp→ Turbidity in 5 min
1° Alcohol + Lucas reagent Room temp→ No turbidity (reacts only on heating)
Turbidity = insoluble alkyl chloride (R–Cl) formed. Reactivity: 3° > 2° > 1° (more stable carbocation)
∴ Lucas test distinguishes all three types quickly at room temperature.
Q 3 Write the Kolbe-Schmitt reaction. What is the product? Give its use. 🔴 5× 3 Marks
Years: 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023 — Name reaction — always asked
Step 1 — Form Sodium Phenoxide
C₆H₅OH + NaOH → C₆H₅ONa + H₂O
Step 2 — React with CO₂
C₆H₅ONa + CO₂ 300 K→4–7 atm Sodium salicylate
Sodium salicylate + HCl → Salicylic acid + NaCl
Product = 2-hydroxybenzoic acid (Salicylic acid). Use: Manufacture of Aspirin (acetylsalicylic acid) — painkiller and antipyretic.
∴ Kolbe-Schmitt: C₆H₅ONa + CO₂ → Salicylic acid (aspirin precursor)
Q 4 Write the Reimer-Tiemann reaction with equation and explain the mechanism. 🔴 5× 3 Marks
Years: 2019, 2020, 2022, 2023, 2024
Reimer-Tiemann Reaction
C₆H₅OH + CHCl₃ + 3KOH aq. KOH→340 K 2-HOC₆H₄CHO + 3KCl + 3H₂O
Product = Salicylaldehyde (2-hydroxybenzaldehyde)
Mechanism (key points):
CHCl₃ + KOH → :CCl₂ (dichlorocarbene — reactive electrophile) :CCl₂ attacks ortho position of phenol ring → intermediate Hydrolysis of intermediate → –CHO group introduced at ortho position
∴ Reimer-Tiemann: Phenol + CHCl₃/KOH → Salicylaldehyde (–CHO at ortho)
Q 5 Why is phenol more acidic than alcohol? Explain with resonance. 🔴 5× 3 Marks
Years: 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023
Acidity Order: Carboxylic acid > Phenol > Water > Alcohol
Reason Phenol > Alcohol:
Phenoxide ion (C₆H₅O⁻) is stabilised by resonance — the negative charge is delocalised over the benzene ring (5 resonance structures: O, ortho-C, ortho-C, para-C, para-C) Alkoxide ion (RO⁻) has NO resonance stabilisation — negative charge stays on O only More stable conjugate base = stronger acid → phenol is more acidic than alcohol
C₆H₅–OH ⇌ C₆H₅–O⁻ + H⁺ pKa = 10
R–OH ⇌ R–O⁻ + H⁺ pKa = 16–18
Lower pKa = stronger acid. Phenol (pKa 10) is 10⁶ times more acidic than ethanol (pKa 16)
∴ Phenol more acidic than alcohol because phenoxide ion is resonance stabilised.
📐 Draw 5 resonance structures of phenoxide ion (C₆H₅O⁻) — O⁻ and 4 ring carbon structures
Q 6 What happens when ethanol reacts with conc. H₂SO₄ at 413 K and 443 K? Write equations. 🔴 5× 3 Marks
Years: 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2024 — Dehydration — repeated every year
At 413 K (140°C) — Intermolecular dehydration → ETHER
2C₂H₅OH conc. H₂SO₄→413 K C₂H₅–O–C₂H₅ + H₂O
At 443 K (170°C) — Intramolecular dehydration → ALKENE
C₂H₅OH conc. H₂SO₄→443 K CH₂=CH₂ + H₂O
⭐ KEY: 413 K → Ether (diethyl ether)  |  443 K → Alkene (ethylene) — Temperature controls the product!
Low temp (413 K) → Ether  |  High temp (443 K) → Alkene
Q 7 Explain Williamson synthesis for preparation of ethers with equation. 🟠 4× 2–3 Marks
Years: 2019, 2021, 2022, 2023
Williamson Synthesis: An ether is prepared by reacting a sodium alkoxide with a primary alkyl halide (SN2 reaction).
General reaction
R–O⁻Na⁺ + R'–X SN2→ R–O–R' + NaX
Example — preparation of methoxyethane
C₂H₅O⁻Na⁺ + CH₃I → C₂H₅–O–CH₃ + NaI
Use PRIMARY alkyl halide only → 3° alkyl halide gives elimination (E2) instead of substitution
∴ Williamson: Na-alkoxide + 1° alkyl halide → Ether (best method for asymmetric ethers)
Q 8 Explain the oxidation of primary, secondary and tertiary alcohols. 🟠 4× 3 Marks
Years: 2020, 2021, 2022, 2024
1° Alcohol → Aldehyde → Carboxylic Acid
RCH₂OH [O] mild→ RCHO [O] excess→ RCOOH
C₂H₅OH K₂Cr₂O₇/H₂SO₄→ CH₃CHO [O]→ CH₃COOH
2° Alcohol → Ketone (stops here)
CH₃CH(OH)CH₃ K₂Cr₂O₇/H⁺→ CH₃COCH₃ (propanone)
3° Alcohol → No oxidation
(CH₃)₃C–OH K₂Cr₂O₇/H⁺→ No reaction (no α-H present)
Oxidising agent: K₂Cr₂O₇/H₂SO₄ or KMnO₄/H⁺ or CrO₃
∴ 1° → aldehyde/acid  |  2° → ketone  |  3° → no reaction
Q 9 Write the reaction of phenol with bromine water. What is the observation? 🟠 4× 2 Marks
Years: 2020, 2021, 2023, 2024
Bromination of Phenol (no catalyst needed)
C₆H₅OH + 3Br₂(aq) Room temp→ 2,4,6-Br₃C₆H₂OH↓ + 3HBr
White precipitate of 2,4,6-tribromophenol formed immediately
Observation: Bromine water is decolourised and a white precipitate is formed immediately.
Why no catalyst? The –OH group activates the benzene ring strongly (strong +M effect) → EAS occurs readily without Lewis acid catalyst (unlike benzene which needs FeBr₃).
∴ Phenol + Br₂/H₂O → White ppt of 2,4,6-tribromophenol (used as test for phenol)
Q 10 Write the reaction of ethers with HI. What are the products? 🟠 4× 2–3 Marks
Years: 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023
Cleavage of Diethyl Ether by HI
With limited HI:
C₂H₅–O–C₂H₅ + HI limited HI→ C₂H₅OH + C₂H₅I
With excess HI:
C₂H₅–O–C₂H₅ + 2HI excess HI→ 2C₂H₅I + H₂O
Reactivity of HX: HI > HBr > HCl  |  Mechanism: SN2 for 1° ether; SN1 for 3° ether
∴ Ether + HI (limited) → alcohol + alkyl iodide  |  Ether + HI (excess) → 2 alkyl iodides

Q 12 Why do alcohols have higher boiling points than ethers of similar molecular mass? 🟢 3× 2 Marks
Years: 2020, 2022, 2023
Reason: Alcohols form intermolecular hydrogen bonds (O–H···O) between molecules due to the O–H group.
Alcohol — H-bonding
R–O–H···O–R | H···O–R (Strong H-bonds) BP: HIGH
C₂H₅OH BP = 78°C
Ether — No H-bonding
R–O–R' No O–H bond No H-bonding between molecules BP: LOW
C₂H₅OC₂H₅ BP = 35°C
∴ Alcohols have higher BP than ethers because alcohols form intermolecular H-bonds; ethers cannot (no O–H).
Q 13 Write the reaction of phenol with NaOH and Na₂CO₃. What do these reactions show? 🟢 3× 2–3 Marks
Years: 2020, 2021, 2024
With NaOH — phenol is acidic
C₆H₅OH + NaOH → C₆H₅ONa + H₂O
With Na₂CO₃ — phenol is stronger than carbonic acid
2C₆H₅OH + Na₂CO₃ → 2C₆H₅ONa + H₂O + CO₂↑
⭐ Phenol reacts with Na₂CO₃ but NOT with NaHCO₃ (pKa phenol = 10 > pKa H₂CO₃ = 6.3 but < pKa HCO₃⁻ = 10.3)
Significance: These reactions prove the acidic nature of phenol. The –OH in phenol ionises to give H⁺, unlike alcohols which do not react with Na₂CO₃.
∴ Phenol is acidic enough to react with Na₂CO₃ but NOT with NaHCO₃
Q 14 What is esterification? Write the reaction of ethanol with acetic acid. 🟢 3× 2 Marks
Years: 2019, 2022, 2024
Esterification: The reaction between a carboxylic acid and an alcohol (in presence of H⁺ catalyst) to form an ester and water. It is a reversible (equilibrium) reaction.
Fischer Esterification
CH₃COOH + C₂H₅OH conc. H₂SO₄⇌Δ CH₃COOC₂H₅ + H₂O
Acetic acid + Ethanol ⇌ Ethyl acetate (fruity smell) + Water · Reversible reaction · H₂SO₄ = catalyst
∴ Esterification: RCOOH + ROH ⇌ RCOOR' + H₂O (H⁺ catalyst, reversible)
Q 15 What is Grignard Reagent? How is it prepared? Write its reactions to prepare 1°, 2° and 3° alcohols. 🔴 5× 3–4 Marks
Years: 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024 — Appeared every year
Grignard Reagent: An organomagnesium halide of general formula R–Mg–X (where R = alkyl/aryl group, X = Cl, Br, I). It is a highly reactive organometallic compound.
Preparation — Alkyl halide + Mg metal in dry ether
R–X + Mg dry ether→ R–Mg–X
C₂H₅Br + Mg dry ether→ C₂H₅MgBr (Ethyl magnesium bromide)
Dry ether essential — anhydrous conditions (Grignard reagent reacts with water). Reactivity: R–I > R–Br > R–Cl
Reactions with carbonyl compounds → Alcohols:
1. With Formaldehyde (HCHO) → PRIMARY Alcohol (1°)
HCHO + RMgX (i) dry ether→(ii) H₃O⁺ RCH₂OH (1° alcohol)
HCHO + C₂H₅MgBr (i) dry ether→(ii) H₃O⁺ C₂H₅CH₂OH (propan-1-ol)
2. With Aldehyde (RCHO) → SECONDARY Alcohol (2°)
RCHO + R'MgX (i) dry ether→(ii) H₃O⁺ RCH(OH)R' (2° alcohol)
CH₃CHO + C₂H₅MgBr (i) dry ether→(ii) H₃O⁺ CH₃CH(OH)C₂H₅ (pentan-2-ol)
3. With Ketone (R₂CO) → TERTIARY Alcohol (3°)
R₂CO + R''MgX (i) dry ether→(ii) H₃O⁺ R₂C(OH)R'' (3° alcohol)
CH₃COCH₃ + C₂H₅MgBr (i) dry ether→(ii) H₃O⁺ (CH₃)₂C(OH)C₂H₅ (2-methylbutan-2-ol)
4. With CO₂ → Carboxylic Acid (not alcohol — bonus)
CO₂ + RMgX (i) dry ether→(ii) H₃O⁺ RCOOH (carboxylic acid)
⭐ KEY: HCHO → 1°  |  RCHO → 2°  |  R₂CO → 3°  |  CO₂ → acid  |  H₂O → R–H (hydrolysis)
Why dry ether is used?
Grignard reagent reacts vigorously with water (moisture): R–Mg–X + H₂O → R–H + Mg(OH)X. So anhydrous (dry) ether is essential as solvent — it dissolves Grignard reagent without reacting.
∴ R–Mg–X is Grignard reagent. HCHO→1°, RCHO→2°, R₂CO→3° alcohols. Always use dry ether and add H₃O⁺ in step 2.
MCQ Most Repeated 1-Mark Questions from Board Papers 🔴 All Repeated 1M each
1. Which gives an immediate turbidity with Lucas reagent? (2019,2020,2022,2023,2024)
Tertiary (3°) alcohol — forms 3° carbocation (most stable) immediately → R–Cl (insoluble, turbid)
2. What is formed when ethanol reacts with conc. H₂SO₄ at 170°C (443 K)? (2019,2020,2021,2022,2023)
Ethylene (CH₂=CH₂) — intramolecular dehydration (elimination)
3. The product of Kolbe-Schmitt reaction is ________. (2019,2021,2022,2023,2024)
Salicylic acid (2-hydroxybenzoic acid) — used to make Aspirin
4. Phenol + Br₂/H₂O → what product? (2020,2021,2022,2024)
2,4,6-tribromophenol (white precipitate) — no catalyst required
5. Which test distinguishes phenol from alcohol? (2019,2020,2023,2024)
FeCl₃ test — phenol gives violet/purple colour; alcohol gives no colour
6. Acidity order: Ethanol, Phenol, Acetic acid (2020,2021,2022,2023)
Ethanol < Phenol < Acetic acid  |  pKa: 16 > 10 > 4.8
7. The reagent used in Williamson synthesis is ________. (2021,2022,2023,2024)
Sodium alkoxide (R–ONa) + Primary alkyl halide → Ether (SN2)
9. Grignard reagent reacts with HCHO to give ________. (2019,2020,2021,2022,2023,2024)
Primary (1°) alcohol — HCHO + RMgX → (i) dry ether (ii) H₃O⁺ → RCH₂OH
10. Why is dry ether used in Grignard reaction? (2020,2021,2022,2023)
Grignard reagent (R–Mg–X) reacts with water: R–Mg–X + H₂O → R–H + Mg(OH)X. So anhydrous (dry) ether is essential — no moisture.
