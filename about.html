Our Story

Visions Learning was founded in 2023 by Shashikant Chauhan in Ambernath with a clear goal — to provide quality coaching to every student in the local community at an affordable price.

Located at Javsai Gaon, Hakre Nagar, Ambernath, we started with a small batch of students and have been growing steadily. Our focus is on personal attention, clear concept building, and helping students score well in board exams, MHT-CET, and competitive exams.

We believe every student in Ambernath deserves the same quality of education as students in big cities. That is the vision behind Visions Learning.

  • Founded in 2023 by Shashikant Chauhan
  • Located at Javsai Gaon, Hakre Nagar, Ambernath
  • Classes for 8th to 12th, MHT-CET and competitive exams
  • Small batches for personal attention to every student
  • Affordable fees with EMI options available
